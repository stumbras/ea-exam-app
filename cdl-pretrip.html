CDL pre-trip inspection checklist

Walk the vehicle in this order. On the test, name each part, point to it or touch it, and tell the examiner what you are checking and why.

How to say it: for most parts the answer has three pieces: it is securely mounted, it is not cracked, bent or broken, and it is not leaking. Add the number when there is one (belt play, tread depth, psi).

Engine compartment (engine off)

Safe start and cab check

Lights and reflectors (walk around)

Brake checks

Steering

Suspension (every axle)

Brakes (every axle)

Wheels (every axle)

Side and rear of the vehicle

Tractor and coupling (combination vehicles)

Trailer

School bus only

Coach or transit bus

Source: vehicle inspection section of the Texas Commercial Motor Vehicle Drivers Handbook (DL-7C, 2026 file), which carries the national CDL manual.
