Frequently asked questions

When can I file?

The IRS usually opens e-filing in late January. You can send your documents earlier, and we file as soon as the IRS accepts returns.

Do I have to mail my passport for an ITIN?

Ask us about your situation before you mail anything. We review your documents with you by secure video and tell you the safest way to submit them.

Is everything online?

Yes. You upload documents to the encrypted client portal, meet by video if you like, and sign electronically. In-person visits are only for ITIN document checks and special cases.

Can you represent me before the IRS now?

We help with IRS letters, notices and payment plans today. Full representation and audit protection open in March 2027; join the waitlist to be first.

Do I have to report crypto?

Simply holding crypto is usually not taxable, but sales, swaps, staking and rewards must be reported. We sort your transactions and report them correctly.

My books are behind. Can you help?

Yes. Catch-up and clean-up work is billed at $40 an hour, and we can keep your books current every month after that.

What should I bring?

Photo ID; Social Security numbers or ITINs for everyone on the return; all income forms (W-2, 1099, W-2G, 1042-S); records of expenses, deductions and estimated payments; last year's return; and bank details for your refund.
