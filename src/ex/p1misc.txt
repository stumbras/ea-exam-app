769|A small credit for people who are 65 or older, or who retired early because of permanent and total disability, and whose income is very low. It is figured on Schedule R and can only reduce tax to zero.
772|A credit for buying a qualifying electric or plug-in vehicle: up to $7,500 new or $4,000 used, with income and price limits. The new law ended it for vehicles acquired after September 30, 2025.
851|A mutual fund sells investments during the year and pays its net long-term gains to shareholders. You report the payment as long-term capital gain no matter how long you have owned the fund shares.
