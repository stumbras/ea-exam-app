Practice questions with answers

1. What score do you need to pass the Utah written knowledge test?

  1. 90% or better
  2. 70% or better
  3. 80% or better
  4. 75% or better
Show answer

C. 80% or better

You must pass with a score of 80% or better.

2. If you are under 18, how long must you hold a Utah learner permit before a license?

  1. One year
  2. Three months
  3. Six months
  4. 30 days
Show answer

C. Six months

Under 18 you need the permit for six months and a completed driver education course.

3. How many hours of driving must a Utah student complete with a parent or guardian?

  1. 40 hours, 10 of them after sunset
  2. 30 hours, 5 after sunset
  3. 50 hours, 10 after sunset
  4. 20 hours
Show answer

A. 40 hours, 10 of them after sunset

The requirement is 40 hours with 10 hours after sunset.

4. On a Utah divided highway with four or more lanes and a median, who must stop for a school bus with flashing red lights?

  1. Only vehicles in the lanes behind the bus
  2. All traffic in both directions
  3. Only the lane next to the bus
  4. No one
Show answer

A. Only vehicles in the lanes behind the bus

With a median separating traffic, only vehicles behind the bus must stop.

5. In Utah you may not park within how many feet of a fire hydrant?

  1. 25 feet
  2. 5 feet
  3. 15 feet
  4. 10 feet
Show answer

C. 15 feet

Parking is not allowed within 15 feet of a fire hydrant.

6. In Utah you may not park within how many feet of a crosswalk?

  1. 20 feet
  2. 30 feet
  3. 15 feet
  4. 10 feet
Show answer

A. 20 feet

Do not park on a crosswalk or within 20 feet of one.

7. In Utah you may not park within how many feet of a flashing beacon, stop sign, yield sign or traffic signal?

  1. 20 feet
  2. 30 feet
  3. 50 feet
  4. 10 feet
Show answer

B. 30 feet

The limit is 30 feet.

8. In Utah you may not park within how many feet of the nearest rail of a railroad crossing?

  1. 30 feet
  2. 15 feet
  3. 50 feet
  4. 100 feet
Show answer

C. 50 feet

The limit is 50 feet.

9. In Utah, dim your high beams before you get within how far of an oncoming car?

  1. 300 feet
  2. 500 feet
  3. 1,000 feet
  4. 100 feet
Show answer

B. 500 feet

Dim before you get within 500 feet of the oncoming car.

10. What following distance rule does the Utah handbook teach?

  1. The five-second rule
  2. The one-second rule
  3. One car length
  4. The two-second rule
Show answer

D. The two-second rule

Use the two-second rule and increase it in bad weather or poor visibility.

11. Utah law requires what minimum distance when passing a bicyclist or other vulnerable user?

  1. One foot
  2. Three feet
  3. Six feet
  4. Two feet
Show answer

B. Three feet

Utah law requires a minimum of three feet.

12. What speed is permitted in Utah when passing a school building or grounds during recess?

  1. 25 miles per hour
  2. 15 miles per hour
  3. 20 miles per hour
  4. 30 miles per hour
Show answer

C. 20 miles per hour

The permitted speed is 20 mph near schools during recess or while children are going to or leaving school.

13. At what age can you get a Utah learner permit?

  1. 16
  2. 15
  3. 14
  4. 15 and a half
Show answer

B. 15

The Utah learner permit is available at 15.

14. Who may sit in the front seat with a Utah learner permit holder?

  1. Any friend with a license
  2. A licensed parent, stepparent, foster parent, guardian, approved instructor, or responsible adult 21 or over
  3. No one is required
  4. Any licensed driver 18 or older
Show answer

B. A licensed parent, stepparent, foster parent, guardian, approved instructor, or responsible adult 21 or over

The front seat passenger must be a licensed parent, stepparent, foster parent, legal guardian, approved driving instructor, or responsible adult 21 or over.

15. How many of the 40 hours of Utah parent-supervised driving must be after sunset?

  1. 10 hours
  2. 20 hours
  3. None
  4. 5 hours
Show answer

A. 10 hours

A student must complete 40 hours of driving, 10 of them after sunset, with a parent or legal guardian.

16. In Utah, which children must be in an approved child safety seat?

  1. Children under five only
  2. Children under twelve
  3. Children under 40 pounds
  4. Children eight and under, unless they are 57 inches tall
Show answer

D. Children eight and under, unless they are 57 inches tall

All children aged eight and under must be in an approved child safety seat unless they are 57 inches tall.
