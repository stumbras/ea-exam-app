Practice questions with answers

1. The Florida Class E Knowledge Exam has how many multiple-choice questions?

  1. 20
  2. 40
  3. 50
  4. 25
Show answer

C. 50

The questions cover Florida traffic laws, safe driving practices and traffic controls.

2. Florida's standard speed limit in a school zone is

  1. 30 mph
  2. 20 mph
  3. 15 mph
  4. 25 mph
Show answer

B. 20 mph

The handbook lists school zones at 20 mph.

3. Florida's standard speed limit on limited access highways is

  1. 65 mph
  2. 75 mph
  3. 70 mph
  4. 55 mph
Show answer

C. 70 mph

Always obey a lower posted limit.

4. In Florida, a driver 21 or older has the license suspended for a breath or blood alcohol level of

  1. .05 or above
  2. .04 or above
  3. .10 or above
  4. .08 or above
Show answer

D. .08 or above

Refusing the test also brings a suspension.

5. Under Florida's Zero Tolerance law, a driver under 21 is suspended for a blood alcohol level of

  1. .01 or higher
  2. .05 or higher
  3. .08 or higher
  4. .02 or higher
Show answer

D. .02 or higher

The suspension is six months. At .05 or higher a substance abuse course is required.

6. In Florida, the first time you refuse a breath, blood or urine test your license is suspended for

  1. Two years
  2. One year
  3. Six months
  4. 30 days
Show answer

B. One year

A second refusal brings an 18-month suspension and a first degree misdemeanor.

7. In Florida, do not use high beams within how far of an oncoming vehicle?

  1. 1,000 feet
  2. 200 feet
  3. 500 feet
  4. 300 feet
Show answer

C. 500 feet

Also switch to low beams within 300 feet of a vehicle ahead of you.

8. In Florida, do not use high beams within how far of the vehicle ahead of you?

  1. 200 feet
  2. 300 feet
  3. 500 feet
  4. 100 feet
Show answer

B. 300 feet

It is 300 feet when following and 500 feet for oncoming vehicles.

9. Florida requires headlights

  1. Between sunset and sunrise
  2. From 30 minutes after sunset
  3. Only after 8 p.m.
  4. Only on highways
Show answer

A. Between sunset and sunrise

Also turn on headlights whenever you turn on your windshield wipers.

10. In Florida, you must signal at least how far before making a turn?

  1. 50 feet
  2. 300 feet
  3. 100 feet
  4. 200 feet
Show answer

C. 100 feet

Signals are also required when changing lanes or passing.

11. In Florida, you may not park within how many feet of a fire hydrant?

  1. 10 feet
  2. 20 feet
  3. 15 feet
  4. 30 feet
Show answer

C. 15 feet

It is 20 feet from an intersection and 30 feet from a stop sign or traffic signal.

12. In Florida, you may not park within how many feet of an intersection?

  1. 30 feet
  2. 20 feet
  3. 15 feet
  4. 10 feet
Show answer

B. 20 feet

The same 20 feet applies to the entrance of a fire, ambulance or rescue station.

13. In Florida, you may not park within how many feet of a stop sign or traffic signal?

  1. 20 feet
  2. 15 feet
  3. 50 feet
  4. 30 feet
Show answer

D. 30 feet

It is 50 feet from a railroad crossing.

14. In Florida, you may not park within how many feet of a railroad crossing?

  1. 50 feet
  2. 15 feet
  3. 20 feet
  4. 30 feet
Show answer

A. 50 feet

Never park where you block or create a hazard for other vehicles.

15. When parked parallel in Florida, your right wheels must be within how far of the curb?

  1. 12 inches
  2. 24 inches
  3. 6 inches
  4. 18 inches
Show answer

A. 12 inches

Park in the direction of traffic, parallel to the curb.

16. Florida law requires you to give a bicyclist at least how much clearance when passing?

  1. Five feet
  2. Three feet
  3. One foot
  4. Two feet
Show answer

B. Three feet

Also slow down and use caution when driving next to bicyclists.

17. A school bus stops ahead on a Florida highway divided by a raised barrier or an unpaved median at least five feet wide. If you are going the opposite direction, you

  1. Must stop for 30 seconds
  2. Must stop until the stop signal is withdrawn
  3. Must pull onto the shoulder
  4. Do not have to stop, but proceed with caution
Show answer

D. Do not have to stop, but proceed with caution

Painted lines are not a barrier. Drivers behind the bus must always stop.

18. A school bus stops on a Florida road divided only by painted lines. Drivers in both directions must

  1. Slow to 10 mph
  2. Stop until the stop signal is withdrawn and children are clear
  3. Proceed with caution
  4. Stop only if children are visible
Show answer

B. Stop until the stop signal is withdrawn and children are clear

Painted lines or pavement markings are not considered barriers.

19. On a Florida multi-lane road, you approach a stopped emergency or tow vehicle and cannot move over. You must slow to

  1. Half the posted limit
  2. 20 mph below the posted speed limit
  3. 10 mph below the posted limit
  4. 5 mph below the posted limit
Show answer

B. 20 mph below the posted speed limit

If the limit is 20 mph or less, travel at 5 mph.

20. Florida's Move Over Law also covers

  1. Only school buses
  2. Only police cars
  3. A disabled vehicle stopped with hazard lights on
  4. Only vehicles on interstates
Show answer

C. A disabled vehicle stopped with hazard lights on

It was expanded to disabled vehicles showing warning lights, flares or signs, or with people visibly present.

21. A Florida driver with a learner's license may drive, for the first three months,

  1. Until midnight
  2. Until 10 p.m.
  3. At any hour
  4. Only during daylight hours
Show answer

D. Only during daylight hours

After three months, driving is allowed until 10 p.m. A licensed driver 21 or older must always be in the front seat.

22. A licensed 16-year-old in Florida may drive alone only between

  1. 6 a.m. and 11 p.m.
  2. 7 a.m. and 9 p.m.
  3. 6 a.m. and 10 p.m.
  4. 5 a.m. and 1 a.m.
Show answer

A. 6 a.m. and 11 p.m.

Exceptions: driving to or from work, or with a licensed driver 21 or older.

23. A licensed 17-year-old in Florida may drive alone only between

  1. 5 a.m. and 10 p.m.
  2. 6 a.m. and 11 p.m.
  3. 6 a.m. and midnight
  4. 5 a.m. and 1 a.m.
Show answer

D. 5 a.m. and 1 a.m.

Exceptions: driving to or from work, or with a licensed driver 21 or older.

24. A Florida driver under 18 must hold a learner's license for

  1. 6 months
  2. 18 months
  3. 3 months
  4. 12 months or until age 18, whichever comes first
Show answer

D. 12 months or until age 18, whichever comes first

The teen also needs 50 hours of driving experience, 10 of them at night.

25. In Florida, how many points within 12 months bring a 30-day license suspension?

  1. 18 points
  2. 8 points
  3. 12 points
  4. 6 points
Show answer

C. 12 points

18 points in 18 months is a 3-month suspension; 24 points in 36 months is one year.

26. In Florida, texting while driving adds how many points to your record?

  1. 3 points
  2. 6 points
  3. 4 points
  4. 1 point
Show answer

A. 3 points

More points are added if the texting was in a school zone or caused a crash.

27. Florida drivers must carry at least

  1. $50,000 combined coverage
  2. $10,000 PIP and $10,000 property damage liability
  3. $5,000 PIP only
  4. $25,000 bodily injury only
Show answer

B. $10,000 PIP and $10,000 property damage liability

Proof of insurance is required to register a vehicle.
