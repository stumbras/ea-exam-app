Practice questions with answers

1. How many questions are on the Pennsylvania knowledge test, and how many must you answer correctly?

  1. 50 questions, 40 correct
  2. 18 questions, 15 correct
  3. 20 questions, 16 correct
  4. 25 questions, 20 correct
Show answer

B. 18 questions, 15 correct

The manual says the Knowledge Test has 18 multiple-choice questions and you must answer 15 correctly.

2. A Pennsylvania permit holder under 18 must complete how many hours of behind-the-wheel practice before the road test?

  1. 50 hours
  2. 40 hours
  3. 100 hours
  4. 65 hours
Show answer

D. 65 hours

Under 18 you need at least 65 hours of supervised skill-building.

3. Of the 65 practice hours for a driver under 18, how many must be at night and in bad weather?

  1. 10 hours at night and 5 hours in bad weather
  2. 20 hours at night and none in bad weather
  3. 5 hours at night and 5 hours in bad weather
  4. 15 hours at night and 10 hours in bad weather
Show answer

A. 10 hours at night and 5 hours in bad weather

The 65 hours must include at least 10 nighttime hours and 5 bad-weather hours.

4. How long must a driver under 18 hold a Pennsylvania learner's permit before taking the road test?

  1. Thirty days
  2. Six months
  3. One year
  4. Three months
Show answer

B. Six months

You must wait the mandatory six months from the permit issue date.

5. A Pennsylvania junior license holder may not drive between which hours without a parent, guardian or spouse 18 or older?

  1. 10 p.m. and 7 a.m.
  2. 9 p.m. and 6 a.m.
  3. Midnight and 4 a.m.
  4. 11 p.m. and 5 a.m.
Show answer

D. 11 p.m. and 5 a.m.

The junior license curfew is 11 p.m. to 5 a.m., with exceptions for work, volunteer fire service and charitable service.

6. A new Pennsylvania junior driver may carry how many passengers under 18 who are not immediate family, without a parent or guardian in the vehicle?

  1. None at any time
  2. No more than one
  3. No more than three
  4. As many as there are seats
Show answer

B. No more than one

The junior license limits you to one passenger under 18 who is not immediate family unless a parent or guardian is with you.

7. In Pennsylvania, when driving under 35 mph, you must signal at least how far before turning?

  1. 50 feet
  2. 300 feet
  3. 100 feet
  4. 200 feet
Show answer

C. 100 feet

Signal at least 100 feet before turning when driving less than 35 mph.

8. In Pennsylvania, when driving 35 mph or faster, you must signal at least how far before turning?

  1. 300 feet
  2. 150 feet
  3. 500 feet
  4. 100 feet
Show answer

A. 300 feet

At 35 mph or more you must signal at least 300 feet before the turn.

9. Pennsylvania law requires low beams when you are within how many feet of an oncoming vehicle?

  1. 500 feet
  2. 1,000 feet
  3. 200 feet
  4. 300 feet
Show answer

A. 500 feet

Use low beams within 500 feet of an oncoming vehicle.

10. Pennsylvania law requires low beams when you are following another vehicle within how many feet?

  1. 300 feet
  2. 500 feet
  3. 100 feet
  4. 150 feet
Show answer

A. 300 feet

Use low beams when following a vehicle within 300 feet.

11. In Pennsylvania you may not park within how many feet of a fire hydrant?

  1. 10 feet
  2. 30 feet
  3. 15 feet
  4. 20 feet
Show answer

C. 15 feet

Parking is not allowed within 15 feet of a fire hydrant.

12. In Pennsylvania you may not park within how many feet of a crosswalk at an intersection?

  1. 15 feet
  2. 30 feet
  3. 20 feet
  4. 10 feet
Show answer

C. 20 feet

Parking is not allowed within 20 feet of a crosswalk at an intersection.

13. In Pennsylvania you may not park within how many feet of a stop sign, yield sign or flashing signal?

  1. 30 feet
  2. 20 feet
  3. 50 feet
  4. 15 feet
Show answer

A. 30 feet

Parking is not allowed within 30 feet of a flashing signal, stop sign, yield sign or traffic control device at the side of the road.

14. In Pennsylvania you may not park within how many feet of the driveway entrance to a fire station?

  1. 30 feet
  2. 20 feet
  3. 10 feet
  4. 50 feet
Show answer

B. 20 feet

The manual lists 20 feet from a fire station driveway entrance.

15. For a driver 21 or older in Pennsylvania, driving under the influence starts at what blood alcohol level?

  1. .05
  2. .08
  3. .04
  4. .10
Show answer

B. .08

At .08 or higher an adult driver is considered to be driving under the influence. You can also be convicted at lower levels if you drive erratically.

16. A Pennsylvania driver under 21 is considered to be driving under the influence at what blood alcohol concentration?

  1. .02 percent or more
  2. .08 percent or more
  3. .04 percent or more
  4. .05 percent or more
Show answer

A. .02 percent or more

Pennsylvania has zero tolerance: .02 or more is DUI for a driver under 21.

17. What happens the first time a Pennsylvania driving record reaches six or more points?

  1. Nothing until 12 points
  2. The driver gets a written notice to take a special point examination
  3. The driver must retake the road test only
  4. The license is cancelled for life
Show answer

B. The driver gets a written notice to take a special point examination

At six or more points for the first time, PennDOT sends a notice to take a written special point examination.

18. A Pennsylvania junior driver gets a mandatory 90-day suspension for which of these?

  1. Six or more points, or driving 26 mph or more over the limit
  2. Any single traffic ticket
  3. Two points on the record
  4. Driving after 9 p.m.
Show answer

A. Six or more points, or driving 26 mph or more over the limit

Junior drivers are suspended for 90 days at six or more points or a conviction for 26 mph or more over the posted limit.

19. What does Pennsylvania's Steer Clear law require when you approach an emergency response area?

  1. Stop in your lane until waved on
  2. Sound your horn and pass
  3. Move into a lane that is not next to it
  4. Speed up to clear the area
Show answer

C. Move into a lane that is not next to it

The Steer Clear law requires moving to a lane that is not adjacent to the emergency response area. If you cannot move over, slow down.

20. When a Pennsylvania school bus stops with red lights flashing and its stop arm out, you must stop at least how far away?

  1. 5 feet
  2. 50 feet
  3. 10 feet
  4. 20 feet
Show answer

C. 10 feet

Stop at least 10 feet from the bus, whether you are behind it, coming toward it, or at an intersection where it is stopped.

21. What is the speed limit in a Pennsylvania school zone when the yellow signals are flashing?

  1. 25 mph
  2. 15 mph
  3. 20 mph
  4. 35 mph
Show answer

B. 15 mph

You may travel no faster than 15 mph when the school zone signals are flashing or during the posted times.

22. Speeding in a Pennsylvania school zone puts how many points on your record?

  1. Three points
  2. Six points
  3. One point
  4. Two points
Show answer

A. Three points

Exceeding the school zone speed limit results in three points.

23. A new Pennsylvania resident with a valid out-of-state license must get a Pennsylvania license within how many days?

  1. 90 days
  2. 10 days
  3. 60 days
  4. 30 days
Show answer

C. 60 days

You must get a Pennsylvania license within 60 days of moving and surrender the out-of-state license.

24. What following distance does the Pennsylvania manual recommend?

  1. 10 seconds
  2. 4 seconds
  3. 1 second
  4. 2 seconds
Show answer

B. 4 seconds

The manual teaches the 4-second rule to give time to steer or brake around a hazard.

25. To avoid overdriving low-beam headlights on a dark road at night, the Pennsylvania manual says not to drive faster than

  1. 65 mph
  2. 55 mph
  3. 45 mph
  4. 35 mph
Show answer

C. 45 mph

Low beams light about 250 feet, so the manual advises no more than 45 mph on a dark road.

26. In Pennsylvania you may not pass within how many feet of an intersection, railroad crossing, bridge or tunnel?

  1. 50 feet
  2. 500 feet
  3. 200 feet
  4. 100 feet
Show answer

D. 100 feet

Passing is prohibited within 100 feet of an intersection or railroad crossing, bridge, elevated structure or tunnel unless a traffic control device allows it.
