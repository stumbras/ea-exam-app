Practice questions with answers

1. What score do you need to pass the Minnesota written test?

  1. 75 percent
  2. 80 percent
  3. 70 percent
  4. 90 percent
Show answer

B. 80 percent

You must score 80 percent, and you may take only one written test per day.

2. What is the minimum age for a Minnesota instruction permit?

  1. 14
  2. 16
  3. 15 and a half
  4. 15
Show answer

D. 15

You must be at least 15 and have completed 30 hours of classroom instruction.

3. How long must a Minnesota driver under 18 hold an instruction permit before a provisional license?

  1. Three months
  2. One year
  3. 30 days
  4. Six months
Show answer

D. Six months

The permit must be held six months without a conviction for a moving, alcohol or controlled substance violation.

4. If you are 19 or older, how long must you hold a Minnesota instruction permit before the road test?

  1. No waiting time
  2. At least six months
  3. At least 30 days
  4. At least three months
Show answer

D. At least three months

Age 19 and older need three months. Age 18 needs six months.

5. How many supervised driving hours must a Minnesota teen log if a parent completed the parent class?

  1. 40 hours, including 15 at night
  2. 20 hours, including 5 at night
  3. 60 hours, including 20 at night
  4. 30 hours, including 10 at night
Show answer

A. 40 hours, including 15 at night

With the parent class the log is 40 hours including 15 nighttime hours. Without it, 50 hours are required.

6. During the first six months of a Minnesota provisional license, you must not drive during which hours without an exception?

  1. Midnight to 5 a.m.
  2. 11 p.m. to 5 a.m.
  3. 1 a.m. to 4 a.m.
  4. 10 p.m. to 6 a.m.
Show answer

A. Midnight to 5 a.m.

No driving from midnight to 5 a.m. unless with a licensed driver 25 or older or driving for work or a school event.

7. During the first six months of a Minnesota provisional license, how many passengers under 20 may you carry without a parent or guardian?

  1. Up to three
  2. None
  3. No limit
  4. Only one
Show answer

D. Only one

Only one passenger under 20 in the first six months, and no more than three in the second six months.

8. May a Minnesota driver under 18 with a permit or provisional license use a hands-free cell phone while driving?

  1. Yes, on highways
  2. No
  3. Yes
  4. Yes, with a parent in the car
Show answer

B. No

Permit and provisional license holders may not use or talk on a cell phone while driving, with or without a hands-free device.

9. In Minnesota, a person can be arrested for DWI with an alcohol concentration of

  1. 0.12 percent or higher
  2. 0.08 percent or higher
  3. 0.05 percent or higher
  4. 0.10 percent or higher
Show answer

B. 0.08 percent or higher

The limit is 0.08 percent, or 0.04 percent when driving a commercial vehicle.

10. When a Minnesota school bus is stopped with red lights flashing, you must stop at least how far from the bus?

  1. 50 feet
  2. 10 feet
  3. 20 feet
  4. 100 feet
Show answer

C. 20 feet

Stop at least 20 feet from the bus, even if the stop arm does not swing out.

11. In Minnesota, the only exception to stopping for a school bus with flashing red lights is

  1. When no children are visible
  2. When you are behind the bus
  3. When the stop arm is not out
  4. On a divided highway when you are traveling in the opposite direction
Show answer

D. On a divided highway when you are traveling in the opposite direction

The only exception is a divided highway with you going the opposite way.

12. Minnesota's Move Over Law requires you, when an emergency vehicle is stopped on a road with two lanes in your direction, to

  1. Keep your lane and speed
  2. Pass on the shoulder
  3. Move to the lane farthest from the vehicle if it is safe
  4. Stop in your lane
Show answer

C. Move to the lane farthest from the vehicle if it is safe

Move a lane away. If you cannot, slow down.

13. In Minnesota you must activate your turn signal at least how far before turning?

  1. 50 feet
  2. 200 feet
  3. 100 feet
  4. 300 feet
Show answer

C. 100 feet

Signal at least 100 feet before turning and keep signaling until the turn is complete.

14. In Minnesota you may not park within how many feet of a fire hydrant?

  1. 15 feet
  2. 5 feet
  3. 20 feet
  4. 10 feet
Show answer

D. 10 feet

Parking is not allowed within 10 feet of a fire hydrant.

15. In Minnesota you may not park within how many feet of a crosswalk at an intersection?

  1. 30 feet
  2. 20 feet
  3. 15 feet
  4. 10 feet
Show answer

B. 20 feet

Parking is not allowed within 20 feet of a crosswalk at an intersection.

16. In Minnesota you may not park within how many feet of a flashing light, stop sign or traffic signal?

  1. 20 feet
  2. 10 feet
  3. 30 feet
  4. 50 feet
Show answer

C. 30 feet

The limit is 30 feet.

17. In Minnesota you may not park within how many feet of the nearest rail of a railroad crossing?

  1. 15 feet
  2. 30 feet
  3. 50 feet
  4. 100 feet
Show answer

C. 50 feet

The limit is 50 feet. It is also illegal to pass within 100 feet of a railroad crossing.

18. When must headlights be on in Minnesota?

  1. Only on highways
  2. Only after 10 p.m.
  3. From sunset to sunrise, in rain, snow, sleet, hail or fog, and when you cannot see 500 feet ahead
  4. Only in winter
Show answer

C. From sunset to sunrise, in rain, snow, sleet, hail or fog, and when you cannot see 500 feet ahead

Turn headlights on at sunset and keep them on until sunrise, and use them in bad weather and low visibility.

19. What following distance rule does the Minnesota manual teach?

  1. The six-second rule
  2. One car length per 10 mph
  3. The three-second rule
  4. The one-second rule
Show answer

C. The three-second rule

The three-second rule keeps a safe distance for standard-length vehicles in good conditions.

20. When passing a bicyclist in Minnesota, you must keep at least

  1. 5 feet
  2. 1 foot
  3. 2 feet
  4. 3 feet between your car and the bicyclist
Show answer

D. 3 feet between your car and the bicyclist

Keep at least 3 feet of space when passing a bicyclist.

21. A new Minnesota resident with a valid out-of-state license has how long to get a Minnesota license?

  1. 90 days
  2. 10 days
  3. Up to 60 days
  4. 30 days
Show answer

C. Up to 60 days

You have up to 60 days after becoming a resident.

22. If you change your name or address in Minnesota, you must apply for a duplicate license within

  1. 90 days
  2. 10 days
  3. 60 days
  4. 30 days
Show answer

D. 30 days

Apply for a duplicate within 30 days.

23. Unless signs say otherwise, what is the Minnesota speed limit on urban or town roads?

  1. 20 mph
  2. 30 mph
  3. 25 mph
  4. 35 mph
Show answer

B. 30 mph

The limits are 10 mph in alleys, 30 mph on urban or town roads and 55 mph in other locations not listed.

24. Unless signs say otherwise, what is the Minnesota speed limit in alleys?

  1. 15 mph
  2. 5 mph
  3. 10 mph
  4. 20 mph
Show answer

C. 10 mph

Alleys are 10 mph.

25. In Minnesota, children under what age must ride in the back seat if one is available?

  1. Under 13
  2. Under 16
  3. Under 10
  4. Under 8
Show answer

A. Under 13

Children under 13 must ride in the back if available. Children under 2 ride rear-facing.

26. In Minnesota, a left turn on a red light is allowed

  1. Only at night
  2. Never
  3. From a one-way road onto a one-way cross street after a complete stop
  4. From any road
Show answer

C. From a one-way road onto a one-way cross street after a complete stop

After a full stop and yielding, you may turn left on red from a one-way road onto a one-way street.
