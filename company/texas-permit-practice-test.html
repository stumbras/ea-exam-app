Practice questions with answers

1. What score do you need to pass a Texas knowledge exam?

  1. 80 percent or better
  2. 70 percent or better
  3. 90 percent or better
  4. 60 percent or better
Show answer

B. 70 percent or better

The handbook says you must score 70 percent or better to pass any knowledge exam.

2. Unless otherwise posted, the speed limit in a Texas urban district is

  1. 35 mph
  2. 25 mph
  3. 30 mph
  4. 20 mph
Show answer

C. 30 mph

The handbook's speed table lists 30 mph for an urban district.

3. Unless otherwise posted, the speed limit in a Texas alley is

  1. 25 mph
  2. 20 mph
  3. 10 mph
  4. 15 mph
Show answer

D. 15 mph

Alleys are 15 mph, the same as beaches and county roads next to a public beach when the county declares it.

4. In Texas, a driver age 21 or over is legally intoxicated with a blood alcohol concentration of

  1. 0.04 or more
  2. 0.05 or more
  3. 0.10 or more
  4. 0.08 or more
Show answer

D. 0.08 or more

Fines can run as high as $10,000, not counting other costs.

5. For a Texas driver under 21, a license suspension can follow from

  1. A BAC of 0.05 or more only
  2. A BAC of 0.08 or more only
  3. A BAC of 0.02 or more only
  4. Any detectable amount of alcohol
Show answer

D. Any detectable amount of alcohol

Texas has zero tolerance for drivers under 21.

6. In Texas, it is illegal to have an open container of alcohol in the passenger area

  1. Only if the driver is drinking
  2. Only while the vehicle is moving
  3. Only at night
  4. Whether the vehicle is moving, stopped or parked on a public highway
Show answer

D. Whether the vehicle is moving, stopped or parked on a public highway

The fine can be up to $500.

7. In Texas, use low beams when you are within how far of an approaching vehicle?

  1. 500 feet
  2. 300 feet
  3. 1,000 feet
  4. 200 feet
Show answer

A. 500 feet

Also use low beams within 300 feet when following another vehicle.

8. In Texas, use low beams when following within how far of another vehicle?

  1. 200 feet
  2. 300 feet
  3. 500 feet
  4. 100 feet
Show answer

B. 300 feet

It is 300 feet when following and 500 feet for approaching vehicles.

9. Texas requires headlights

  1. Only outside cities
  2. From 30 minutes after sunset to 30 minutes before sunrise
  3. Only after 9 p.m.
  4. From sunset to sunrise
Show answer

B. From 30 minutes after sunset to 30 minutes before sunrise

Also use them any time people or vehicles cannot be seen clearly for at least 1,000 feet.

10. In Texas, give a turn signal at least how far before you turn?

  1. 300 feet
  2. 50 feet
  3. 100 feet
  4. 200 feet
Show answer

C. 100 feet

Signal at least 100 feet before the turn.

11. In Texas, you may not park within how many feet of a fire hydrant?

  1. 20 feet
  2. 30 feet
  3. 15 feet
  4. 10 feet
Show answer

C. 15 feet

It is 15 feet from a hydrant and 20 feet from a crosswalk at an intersection.

12. In Texas, you may not park within how many feet of a crosswalk at an intersection?

  1. 30 feet
  2. 15 feet
  3. 10 feet
  4. 20 feet
Show answer

D. 20 feet

It is 30 feet on the approach to a stop sign, yield sign or traffic signal.

13. In Texas, you may not park within how many feet of the approach to a stop sign or traffic signal?

  1. 30 feet
  2. 50 feet
  3. 20 feet
  4. 15 feet
Show answer

A. 30 feet

The rule covers flashing signals, stop signs, yield signs and other traffic signals at the side of the road.

14. In Texas, you may not park within how many feet of the nearest rail of a railroad crossing?

  1. 20 feet
  2. 15 feet
  3. 50 feet
  4. 100 feet
Show answer

C. 50 feet

This applies whether or not the vehicle is occupied.

15. When a Texas railroad signal warns of a train, you must stop how far from the nearest rail?

  1. At least 200 feet
  2. Between 5 and 10 feet
  3. Between 15 and 50 feet
  4. Between 50 and 100 feet
Show answer

C. Between 15 and 50 feet

Stay stopped until it is safe or you are allowed to proceed.

16. A Texas school bus is stopped with alternately flashing red lights. You must

  1. Stop, whichever direction you are coming from
  2. Slow to 10 mph and pass
  3. Stop only if you are behind the bus
  4. Honk and pass on the left
Show answer

A. Stop, whichever direction you are coming from

Wait until the bus moves, the driver signals you on, or the red lights stop flashing.

17. In Texas, you do not have to stop for a school bus with flashing red lights when

  1. You are driving the opposite way on a two-lane road
  2. The bus is in a school zone
  3. You are on a different road or a controlled-access highway
  4. It is after dark
Show answer

C. You are on a different road or a controlled-access highway

In all other cases, stop from either direction.

18. You approach a stopped emergency vehicle with lights on in Texas and cannot change lanes. Where the limit is 25 mph or more, you must slow to

  1. 20 mph less than the posted speed limit
  2. 5 mph less than the posted limit
  3. 10 mph less than the posted limit
  4. The posted limit
Show answer

A. 20 mph less than the posted speed limit

Where the limit is under 25 mph, slow to less than 5 mph.

19. A Texas learner license applicant under 18 must be at least

  1. 16 years old
  2. 15½ years old
  3. 14 years old
  4. 15 years old
Show answer

D. 15 years old

The applicant must also complete the classroom part of driver education.

20. A Texas driver under 18 must hold a learner license for at least how long before getting a provisional license?

  1. Three months
  2. Nine months
  3. Six months
  4. Twelve months
Show answer

C. Six months

A suspension during this phase extends the six months.

21. In Phase Two of the Texas graduated license, a driver under 18 may not drive between

  1. 1:00 a.m. and 4:00 a.m.
  2. 11:00 p.m. and 5:00 a.m.
  3. Midnight and 5:00 a.m.
  4. 10:00 p.m. and 6:00 a.m.
Show answer

C. Midnight and 5:00 a.m.

Exceptions are work, school activities and medical emergencies.

22. In Phase Two of the Texas graduated license, a driver under 18 may carry no more than

  1. Three passengers of any age
  2. No passengers at all
  3. One passenger under 21 who is not a family member
  4. Two passengers under 21
Show answer

C. One passenger under 21 who is not a family member

Family members do not count toward the limit.

23. Texas drivers under 18 may use a wireless communication device

  1. Any time with a hands-free device
  2. Only at red lights
  3. Only in an emergency, and hands-free devices are also banned
  4. Only on highways
Show answer

C. Only in an emergency, and hands-free devices are also banned

The restriction covers hands-free devices too.

24. The minimum liability insurance in Texas is

  1. $25,000 / $50,000 / $25,000
  2. $50,000 / $100,000 / $50,000
  3. $15,000 / $30,000 / $5,000
  4. $30,000 per person, $60,000 per crash, $25,000 property damage
Show answer

D. $30,000 per person, $60,000 per crash, $25,000 property damage

This is often written 30/60/25.

25. The fine for a first conviction of driving without a license in Texas is

  1. Up to $50
  2. Up to $200
  3. Up to $1,000
  4. Up to $500
Show answer

B. Up to $200

A second conviction within a year is $25 to $200.
