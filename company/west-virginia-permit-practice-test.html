Practice questions with answers

1. What is the minimum age for a West Virginia Level 1 GDL instruction permit?

  1. 15 and a half
  2. 16
  3. 14
  4. 15
Show answer

D. 15

You must be at least 15, pass a vision screening and knowledge test, and have a School Driver Eligibility Certificate.

2. Who must ride with a West Virginia Level 1 permit holder?

  1. Any licensed driver 18 or older
  2. A licensed driver at least 21 in the right front seat
  3. Any adult in the car
  4. A driving instructor only
Show answer

B. A licensed driver at least 21 in the right front seat

You may only drive with a licensed driver at least 21 years old in the right front passenger seat.

3. During which hours may a West Virginia Level 1 permit holder drive?

  1. 5:00 AM to 10:00 PM
  2. 5:00 AM to midnight
  3. 6:00 AM to 9:00 PM
  4. Any time with a supervisor
Show answer

A. 5:00 AM to 10:00 PM

Level 1 permit holders may only drive between 5:00 AM and 10:00 PM.

4. How long must a West Virginia permit holder drive conviction-free before a Level 2 intermediate license?

  1. 1 year
  2. 30 days
  3. 180 consecutive days
  4. 90 days
Show answer

C. 180 consecutive days

You need at least 180 consecutive days of conviction-free driving at Level 1 before applying for Level 2.

5. How much supervised driving must be certified for a West Virginia Level 2 license (if no driver's ed course)?

  1. 100 hours
  2. 50 hours, including 10 at night
  3. 40 hours, including 5 at night
  4. 30 hours
Show answer

B. 50 hours, including 10 at night

A parent or guardian must certify at least 50 hours behind the wheel, including 10 at night, or you complete an approved driver's education course.

6. During the first six months of a West Virginia Level 2 intermediate license, how many non-family passengers under 20 may ride along?

  1. Two
  2. One
  3. Three
  4. None
Show answer

D. None

For the first six months, no non-family passengers under 20; during the second six months, no more than one.

7. What is the West Virginia blood alcohol limit for drivers under 21?

  1. 0.05%
  2. 0.04%
  3. 0.08%
  4. 0.02%
Show answer

D. 0.02%

It is against the law to drive at 0.08% or higher if 21 or older, and at 0.02% or higher if under 21.

8. When may traffic coming toward a stopped West Virginia school bus keep going?

  1. When no children are visible
  2. Only on interstate highways, when the bus is on the other side of the median
  3. On any four-lane road
  4. Never
Show answer

B. Only on interstate highways, when the bus is on the other side of the median

Traffic in both directions must stop; only on interstates does traffic on the other side of the median not have to stop.

9. How close may you park to a fire hydrant in West Virginia?

  1. No closer than 15 feet
  2. No closer than 5 feet
  3. No closer than 20 feet
  4. No closer than 10 feet
Show answer

A. No closer than 15 feet

Do not park within 15 feet of a fire hydrant.

10. How close may you park to a stop sign or traffic signal in West Virginia?

  1. No closer than 15 feet
  2. No closer than 30 feet
  3. No closer than 50 feet
  4. No closer than 20 feet
Show answer

B. No closer than 30 feet

Do not park within 30 feet of any stop sign or traffic control signal.

11. How close may you park to the nearest rail of a railroad crossing in West Virginia?

  1. No closer than 30 feet
  2. No closer than 50 feet
  3. No closer than 15 feet
  4. No closer than 100 feet
Show answer

B. No closer than 50 feet

Do not park within 50 feet of the nearest rail of a railroad crossing.

12. When must you dim your headlights in West Virginia?

  1. Only on city streets
  2. Within 300 feet of any vehicle
  3. Only when the other driver dims
  4. Within 500 feet of an approaching vehicle and within 200 feet of a vehicle ahead
Show answer

D. Within 500 feet of an approaching vehicle and within 200 feet of a vehicle ahead

Dim within 500 feet of an approaching vehicle and within 200 feet of the vehicle in front of you.

13. What following-distance rule does the West Virginia handbook recommend?

  1. Ten seconds
  2. One second
  3. Two car lengths at any speed
  4. Three-to-four seconds
Show answer

D. Three-to-four seconds

Use the three-to-four second rule, counting from when the vehicle ahead passes a fixed object.

14. In West Virginia, what is the slowest you should drive on an interstate in good conditions?

  1. 50 mph
  2. 55 mph
  3. 45 mph
  4. 40 mph
Show answer

B. 55 mph

Do not drive slower than 55 mph on an interstate unless weather or road conditions are bad or a lower speed is posted.

15. A child under 8 in West Virginia may use a seat belt instead of a child seat when the child is how tall?

  1. At least 4 feet 9 inches
  2. At least 4 feet
  3. At least 5 feet
  4. At least 3 feet 6 inches
Show answer

A. At least 4 feet 9 inches

If a child under 8 is at least 4 feet 9 inches tall, a safety belt is sufficient.

16. Within how many days must you notify the West Virginia DMV of an address change?

  1. 20 days
  2. 30 days
  3. 10 days
  4. 60 days
Show answer

A. 20 days

If you change your address you must notify the DMV in writing within 20 days.

17. New residents must apply for a West Virginia driver's license within how many days?

  1. 60 days
  2. 30 days
  3. 10 days
  4. 90 days
Show answer

B. 30 days

New residents who want to drive in West Virginia must apply for a WV license within 30 days.
