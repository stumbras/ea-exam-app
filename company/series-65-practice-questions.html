Free Series 65 Practice Questions with Answers and Explanations

The Series 65 is the NASAA Uniform Investment Adviser Law Examination. It has 130 scored questions plus 10 unscored trial questions, 180 minutes, and you need 92 of 130 (about 71%) to pass. The fee is $187. No firm sponsor and no experience are required.

Below are 30 free practice questions. Pick your answer, then tap Show answer.

1. Which is an example of expansionary fiscal policy?

  1. Congress raises individual income tax rates
  2. The Fed raises bank reserve requirements
  3. Congress raises government spending
  4. The Fed sells Treasury bills in the open market
Show answer

C. Congress raises government spending

Fiscal policy is taxing and spending by Congress and the President. Reserve requirements and open-market operations are monetary policy tools of the Federal Reserve. Source: uploaded PASS READY document.

2. Which statement about standard deviation is TRUE?

  1. It is the same as beta
  2. It measures only downside risk
  3. About 68% of outcomes fall within one standard deviation of the mean in a normal distribution
  4. A higher standard deviation means less risk, because returns are spread over a wider range
Show answer

C. About 68% of outcomes fall within one standard deviation of the mean in a normal distribution

About 95% fall within two standard deviations.

3. A high debt-to-equity ratio usually signals:

  1. Lower profit margins for the year
  2. Higher liquidity for the company
  3. Higher financial risk from leverage
  4. Lower leverage and lower risk
Show answer

C. Higher financial risk from leverage

More debt means fixed interest payments that must be met in bad years as well as good. Source: uploaded PASS READY document.

4. The internal rate of return (IRR) is the discount rate that

  1. Makes an investment's net present value equal zero
  2. Makes the investment's future value double
  3. Makes the investment's payback period one year
  4. Equals the yield on 10-year Treasury notes
Show answer

A. Makes an investment's net present value equal zero

If IRR exceeds the required return, the investment is attractive.

5. Credit spreads between BBB corporate bonds and Treasuries narrow sharply. This most likely signals

  1. rising default rates
  2. fear of a coming recession in the economy
  3. a flight to Treasuries
  4. investor confidence in the economy
Show answer

D. investor confidence in the economy

Widening spreads signal fear.

6. A stock sells for $48, earns $3.00 per share and pays a $1.20 dividend. Its price-to-earnings ratio is

  1. 6.25
  2. 40
  3. 2.5%
  4. 16
Show answer

D. 16

$48 ÷ $3.00 = 16. The dividend is not part of the P/E ratio; $1.20 ÷ $48 = 2.5% is the dividend yield.

7. Which section of the statement of cash flows shows money paid out as dividends?

  1. Investing activities
  2. The income statement
  3. Operating activities
  4. Financing activities
Show answer

D. Financing activities

Dividends, borrowing and repaying debt, issuing and buying back stock are financing.

8. Real GDP fell for two straight quarters while unemployment rose. Which policy response is MOST likely from the Fed?

  1. Raising the discount rate
  2. Selling Treasury securities
  3. Raising reserve requirements for all banks
  4. Lowering interest rates by buying securities
Show answer

D. Lowering interest rates by buying securities

In a recession with low inflation, the Fed eases: it buys securities in open market operations, which adds reserves and pushes interest rates down.

9. FDIC insurance covers bank deposits up to

  1. Unlimited amounts in savings accounts at any member bank
  2. $250,000 per depositor, per insured bank, per ownership category
  3. $100,000 per bank for all of a person's accounts combined
  4. $500,000 per account, including stocks held at the bank
Show answer

B. $250,000 per depositor, per insured bank, per ownership category

Brokered CDs are FDIC-insured within those limits.

10. Which statement about a floating-rate bond is TRUE?

  1. Its price stays near par because its coupon resets with market rates
  2. It always pays a fixed coupon, so its price swings the most
  3. It has the highest duration
  4. It is a zero-coupon bond
Show answer

A. Its price stays near par because its coupon resets with market rates

Low interest rate risk; credit risk remains.

11. Investors in a master limited partnership (MLP) usually receive

  1. A Form 1099-DIV only
  2. A Schedule K-1 for tax reporting
  3. No tax form at all
  4. A Form W-2 from the partnership
Show answer

B. A Schedule K-1 for tax reporting

MLPs are pass-through entities, often in energy pipelines.

12. A preferred stock pays a fixed $5 dividend and trades at $100. Rates in the market rise to 6% for similar preferreds. The price will most likely

  1. rise to about $120
  2. fall to $50
  3. fall to about $83
  4. stay at $100
Show answer

C. fall to about $83

$5 ÷ 6% ≈ $83. Straight preferred trades like a perpetual bond.

13. Which life insurance policy lets the owner change premium payments and the death benefit, and invest the cash value in subaccounts?

  1. Variable universal life
  2. Term life
  3. Whole life
  4. Universal life (fixed)
Show answer

A. Variable universal life

Universal gives flexibility; variable adds subaccounts and makes it a security.

14. What is the key risk of holding a long-duration bond relative to a short-duration bond?

  1. Greater price sensitivity to yield changes
  2. Automatic inflation protection
  3. Less exposure to interest rates
  4. No default risk
Show answer

A. Greater price sensitivity to yield changes

Duration approximates percentage price change per one-point yield change. Source: NASAA outline: duration.

15. Collateralized mortgage obligations (CMOs) divide mortgage cash flows into

  1. Tranches with different maturities and risks
  2. Separate policies insured by the FHA
  3. Equal slices with identical maturities
  4. Stock and bond portions with fixed rates
Show answer

A. Tranches with different maturities and risks

Tranching spreads prepayment risk differently among investors.

16. Which statement about fixed indexed annuities is TRUE?

  1. They have no surrender charges
  2. They are always securities
  3. The owner directly owns the index stocks and receives all of their dividends each quarter
  4. They credit interest linked to an index, with a floor and a cap, and are generally not securities
Show answer

D. They credit interest linked to an index, with a floor and a cap, and are generally not securities

Participation rates and caps limit the upside.

17. A 401(k) participant aged 62 in 2026 wants to save as much as possible. Her elective deferral limit, including catch-up, is

  1. $8,000 only
  2. $7,500
  3. higher than at 50 to 59, because of the special catch-up for ages 60 to 63
  4. $24,500 only, with no catch-up contribution allowed at all from ages 60 to 63
Show answer

C. higher than at 50 to 59, because of the special catch-up for ages 60 to 63

SECURE 2.0 created a larger catch-up for ages 60 to 63.

18. A client asks why her Roth IRA conversion last year did not trigger the 10% penalty, but a withdrawal of the converted money this year at age 50 did. The reason is

  1. conversions are taxed twice
  2. converted amounts withdrawn within 5 years before 59½ are subject to the penalty
  3. Roth IRAs always charge a 10% penalty on any withdrawal at all before age 59½
  4. the IRS made an error
Show answer

B. converted amounts withdrawn within 5 years before 59½ are subject to the penalty

Each conversion has its own 5-year clock for the penalty.

19. A stock bought at $40 pays $2 in dividends and is sold a year later at $44. The holding period return is

  1. 13.6%
  2. 5%
  3. 10%
  4. 15%
Show answer

D. 15%

($44 − $40 + $2) ÷ $40 = 15%.

20. A 529 beneficiary receives a full scholarship. The owner withdraws an amount equal to the scholarship. The earnings portion is

  1. taxable, plus the full 10% penalty
  2. forfeited
  3. taxable, but the 10% penalty is waived
  4. tax-free
Show answer

C. taxable, but the 10% penalty is waived

The scholarship exception waives only the penalty.

21. A client has a $50,000 capital loss carryforward and $10,000 of new gains this year with no other gains. How much loss may she deduct against ordinary income this year?

  1. $10,000
  2. $3,000
  3. $50,000
  4. $0
Show answer

B. $3,000

Losses first offset the $10,000 gain, then up to $3,000 of ordinary income; $37,000 carries forward.

22. Which account type lets a donor keep control, change the beneficiary to another family member and get tax-free growth for education?

  1. UTMA
  2. Coverdell ESA for a 31-year-old
  3. Roth IRA in the child's name
  4. A 529 plan
Show answer

D. A 529 plan

Coverdell beneficiaries must use or roll the money by 30.

23. A client says she wants growth but panics and sells whenever the market falls 10%. The adviser should

  1. ignore the behavior and buy aggressive growth funds anyway
  2. stop advising her
  3. recommend leveraged ETFs
  4. treat her real risk tolerance as low and discuss it
Show answer

D. treat her real risk tolerance as low and discuss it

Behavior reveals risk tolerance more reliably than stated goals.

24. A portfolio set at 60% stocks and 40% bonds is rebalanced back to those weights each year. This is

  1. market timing
  2. tactical asset allocation
  3. sector rotation
  4. strategic asset allocation
Show answer

D. strategic asset allocation

Long-term targets with rebalancing. Tactical means temporary shifts away from the targets.

25. An adviser wants to sell bonds from its own account to an advisory client. It must

  1. get consent once a year
  2. disclose its capacity in writing and get the client's consent before the trade is completed
  3. report the trade to the Administrator within 10 days, with no client consent needed
  4. charge no advisory fee that quarter
Show answer

B. disclose its capacity in writing and get the client's consent before the trade is completed

Principal trades need disclosure and consent trade by trade.

26. A customer deposits $4,000 in cash on Monday, $4,000 on Tuesday and $4,500 on Wednesday to avoid a report. The firm should

  1. file a suspicious activity report
  2. file three currency transaction reports
  3. tell the customer a report is being filed
  4. do nothing; each deposit is under $10,000
Show answer

A. file a suspicious activity report

This is structuring. A SAR is filed and the customer is not told.

27. Which is TRUE of the Investment Advisers Act of 1940 compared with the Uniform Securities Act?

  1. Only the Uniform Securities Act prohibits fraud
  2. The 1940 Act does not apply to state-registered advisers at all, so only state law covers them
  3. The 1940 Act requires registration of IARs with the SEC
  4. Both prohibit fraud by advisers; the 1940 Act's antifraud rule covers advisers whether or not registered
Show answer

D. Both prohibit fraud by advisers; the 1940 Act's antifraud rule covers advisers whether or not registered

Section 206 antifraud provisions apply to all advisers, including those exempt from registration.

28. An adviser suspects that an 80-year-old client's nephew is draining her account. Under the NASAA model act the firm may

  1. do nothing without a court order
  2. close the account and send a check
  3. refuse all trading in the account for a year, without telling anyone about the concern
  4. delay the disbursement and notify the state securities regulator and adult protective services
Show answer

D. delay the disbursement and notify the state securities regulator and adult protective services

A delay of up to 15 business days, with immunity for good-faith reports.

29. An adviser shows hypothetical performance in an advertisement. Under the 2026 recordkeeping amendments, what must it also record?

  1. Every reader's name and address
  2. Who the intended audience is
  3. The ad's printing costs
  4. The Administrator's approval
Show answer

B. Who the intended audience is

Hypothetical performance must be relevant to the likely financial situation of the intended audience.

30. Which security is a federal covered security?

  1. A security in a Rule 504 offering
  2. A stock sold in an intrastate offering under Rule 147
  3. Common stock listed on the New York Stock Exchange
  4. A penny stock traded over the counter
Show answer

C. Common stock listed on the New York Stock Exchange

Exchange-listed securities, investment company securities and Rule 506 offerings are federal covered.
