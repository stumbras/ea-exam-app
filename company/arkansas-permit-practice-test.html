Practice questions with answers

1. What is the minimum age for an Arkansas instruction permit?

  1. 16
  2. 13
  3. 14
  4. 15
Show answer

C. 14

To apply for an instruction permit the applicant must be at least 14 years of age and pass the knowledge exam and vision test.

2. Who may supervise an Arkansas instruction permit holder?

  1. Any adult passenger
  2. A licensed driver 21 or older sitting beside the driver
  3. Any licensed driver 18 or older
  4. A licensed driver anywhere in the car
Show answer

B. A licensed driver 21 or older sitting beside the driver

The permit holder may drive with a licensed driver 21 years of age or older who is occupying the seat beside the driver.

3. How long is an Arkansas instruction permit issued for?

  1. 5 years
  2. 6 months
  3. 2 years
  4. 1 year
Show answer

C. 2 years

The instruction permit is issued for two years.

4. Which Arkansas license is the restricted license for drivers aged 14 to 16?

  1. Learner's License
  2. Class MD License
  3. Intermediate License
  4. Class D License
Show answer

A. Learner's License

The Learner's License is a restricted license issued to persons between 14 and 16 years of age.

5. Which Arkansas license is the restricted license for drivers aged 16 to 18?

  1. Instruction Permit
  2. Learner's License
  3. Class M License
  4. Intermediate License
Show answer

D. Intermediate License

The Intermediate License is a restricted license issued to persons between 16 and 18 years of age.

6. Under an Arkansas Learner's or Intermediate License, when may the driver use a cell phone?

  1. When stopped at a red light
  2. Only in an emergency
  3. Hands-free at any time
  4. Never, not even in an emergency
Show answer

B. Only in an emergency

Talking or texting on a cell phone is prohibited by law except for an emergency.

7. Before getting a Learner's License in Arkansas, how long must the applicant be free of crashes and serious traffic violations?

  1. 1 year
  2. 3 months
  3. 6 months
  4. 30 days
Show answer

C. 6 months

The applicant cannot be involved in a crash or convicted of a serious traffic violation within the six months before applying.

8. When must you stop for a school bus with flashing red lights in Arkansas?

  1. Only inside city limits
  2. Whether you are behind it, coming toward it or at an intersection, unless a median 20 feet or wider separates you from an oncoming bus
  3. Only when you are behind it
  4. Only on two-lane roads
Show answer

B. Whether you are behind it, coming toward it or at an intersection, unless a median 20 feet or wider separates you from an oncoming bus

You must stop no matter where you are, except you need not stop for a bus in an opposite lane separated by a median 20 feet or more wide.

9. Within what distance of an oncoming vehicle must you dim your high beams in Arkansas?

  1. 500 feet
  2. 100 feet
  3. 200 feet
  4. 1,000 feet
Show answer

A. 500 feet

Dim your high beams whenever you come within 500 feet of an oncoming vehicle, and use low beams when following closer than 200 feet.

10. When does Arkansas law require headlights to be on?

  1. Only in fog
  2. Only after dark in cities
  3. When windshield wipers are needed, and from 30 minutes after sunset to 30 minutes before sunrise
  4. Only on interstates
Show answer

C. When windshield wipers are needed, and from 30 minutes after sunset to 30 minutes before sunrise

Arkansas law requires headlights when windshield wipers are needed and from half an hour after sunset to half an hour before sunrise.

11. In Arkansas, which children must ride in a child passenger safety seat?

  1. Under 80 pounds regardless of age
  2. Under 2 years old only
  3. Under 12 years old
  4. Under 6 years old and under 60 pounds
Show answer

D. Under 6 years old and under 60 pounds

A child less than six years of age who weighs less than 60 pounds must be restrained in a properly secured child passenger safety seat.

12. What is the most a driver can be fined for a first Arkansas seat belt violation?

  1. $100
  2. $250
  3. No fine, only a warning
  4. $25 plus court costs
Show answer

D. $25 plus court costs

A violation brings a fine not to exceed $25, plus any court costs or fees. Police may stop you for a seat belt violation alone.

13. What must you do when an emergency vehicle with a siren and flashing red or blue lights approaches in Arkansas?

  1. Speed up to clear the way
  2. Pull over to the right edge of the road
  3. Move to the left lane
  4. Stop exactly where you are
Show answer

B. Pull over to the right edge of the road

Arkansas law requires you to move over to the right edge of the road, or as near to the right as possible.

14. What happens to fines for moving violations in an Arkansas highway work zone?

  1. They are doubled when workers and advance warning signs are present
  2. They are waived for first offenses
  3. They are tripled at all times
  4. They stay the same
Show answer

A. They are doubled when workers and advance warning signs are present

Fines for all moving traffic violations in a work zone are doubled if workers are present and warning signs are posted in advance.

15. On an Arkansas multilane highway, when may you drive in the left lane?

  1. Whenever you are going the speed limit
  2. Only at night
  3. Any time traffic is light
  4. To pass, or when the other lanes are closed or blocked
Show answer

D. To pass, or when the other lanes are closed or blocked

State law says the left lane of a multilane highway is for passing, with exceptions such as other lanes being closed for construction.

16. How fast does a closed car heat up on a 90-degree day, according to the Arkansas guide?

  1. It stays cooler than outside for an hour
  2. It reaches the outside temperature within 5 minutes and keeps rising
  3. It heats up only if the windows are dark
  4. It takes about 2 hours
Show answer

B. It reaches the outside temperature within 5 minutes and keeps rising

Within five minutes the inside reaches the outdoor temperature, and it keeps climbing. Never leave children or pets unattended in a vehicle.
