Practice questions with answers

1. How many questions must you answer correctly to pass each Georgia knowledge exam?

  1. 12 out of 20
  2. 15 out of 20
  3. 20 out of 25
  4. 18 out of 20
Show answer

B. 15 out of 20

The manual says you must correctly answer 15 out of 20 questions to pass. The road signs and road rules tests are scored separately.

2. Before a Georgia Class D license, a teen must have how many hours of supervised driving, and how many at night?

  1. 30 hours, including 5 at night
  2. 65 hours, including 10 at night
  3. 40 hours, including 6 at night
  4. 50 hours, including 10 at night
Show answer

C. 40 hours, including 6 at night

Georgia requires at least 40 hours of supervised driving, six of them at night.

3. A Georgia Class D provisional license holder may not drive between which hours?

  1. 1:00 a.m. and 4:00 a.m.
  2. 12:00 a.m. and 5:00 a.m., with no exceptions
  3. 10:00 p.m. and 6:00 a.m.
  4. 11:00 p.m. and 5:00 a.m., except for work
Show answer

B. 12:00 a.m. and 5:00 a.m., with no exceptions

Class D holders may not drive between midnight and 5:00 a.m. The manual says there are no exceptions.

4. During the first six months of a Georgia Class D license, who may ride with the driver?

  1. Any passengers under 21
  2. Only immediate family members
  3. One friend of any age
  4. Anyone, if all wear seat belts
Show answer

B. Only immediate family members

For the first six months after issuance only immediate family members may ride in the vehicle.

5. A person supervising a Georgia instructional permit holder must be at least what age and licensed for that class of vehicle?

  1. 18
  2. 21
  3. 19
  4. 25
Show answer

B. 21

The permit holder must be accompanied by a person at least 21 who is licensed for a Class C vehicle and seated beside the driver.

6. In Georgia you may not park within how many feet of a fire hydrant?

  1. 10 feet
  2. 20 feet
  3. 15 feet
  4. 30 feet
Show answer

C. 15 feet

Parking is prohibited within 15 feet of a fire hydrant.

7. In Georgia you may not park within how many feet of a crosswalk at an intersection?

  1. 10 feet
  2. 20 feet
  3. 30 feet
  4. 15 feet
Show answer

B. 20 feet

Parking is prohibited within 20 feet of a crosswalk at an intersection.

8. In Georgia you may not park within how many feet of a stop sign, yield sign or traffic signal?

  1. 15 feet
  2. 50 feet
  3. 20 feet
  4. 30 feet
Show answer

D. 30 feet

Parking is prohibited within 30 feet of a stop sign, yield sign or traffic control signal.

9. In Georgia you may not park within how many feet of a railroad crossing?

  1. 100 feet
  2. 50 feet
  3. 30 feet
  4. 15 feet
Show answer

B. 50 feet

Parking is prohibited within 50 feet of a railroad crossing.

10. When a Georgia railroad crossing signal is flashing or the gates are lowered, where must you stop?

  1. Within 50 feet, but not less than 15 feet, from the nearest rail
  2. Right at the gate
  3. At least 100 feet away
  4. Anywhere before the tracks
Show answer

A. Within 50 feet, but not less than 15 feet, from the nearest rail

You must stop within 50 feet but not less than 15 feet from the nearest rail.

11. In Georgia you must dim your high beams within how many feet of an approaching vehicle?

  1. 300 feet
  2. 1,000 feet
  3. 500 feet
  4. 200 feet
Show answer

C. 500 feet

Use low beams within 500 feet of an approaching vehicle, and when following within 200 feet.

12. In Georgia you must dim your high beams when following another vehicle within how many feet?

  1. 300 feet
  2. 500 feet
  3. 200 feet
  4. 100 feet
Show answer

C. 200 feet

Dim your lights when following closely, within 200 feet.

13. For a Georgia driver 21 or older, what blood alcohol level is considered under the influence?

  1. 0.08 or more
  2. 0.05 or more
  3. 0.04 or more
  4. 0.10 or more
Show answer

A. 0.08 or more

A driver is considered under the influence when 0.08 grams or more by alcohol weight is present in the blood.

14. For a Georgia driver under 21, what blood alcohol level is considered under the influence?

  1. 0.08 or more
  2. 0.00 only
  3. 0.02 or more
  4. 0.05 or more
Show answer

C. 0.02 or more

For drivers under 21 the limit is 0.02.

15. A Georgia driver under 21 faces suspension for accumulating how many points within twelve months?

  1. 2 points
  2. 8 points
  3. 15 points
  4. 4 points
Show answer

D. 4 points

Drivers under 21 are suspended for accumulating 4 points within twelve months.

16. What does Georgia's Hands-Free Law prohibit?

  1. Using a mounted phone for navigation
  2. Talking through a wireless headset
  3. Listening to music
  4. Holding or supporting a phone for any reason while driving
Show answer

D. Holding or supporting a phone for any reason while driving

Drivers may not hold or support a phone. A phone may be used with headphones, a wireless device, a phone holder or a mounted device.

17. What is the minimum legal space for a Georgia motorist passing a bicyclist?

  1. 3 feet
  2. 10 feet
  3. 5 feet
  4. 1 foot
Show answer

A. 3 feet

The minimum legal space to pass a bicyclist is 3 feet.

18. In Georgia, a child under what age must ride in an appropriate child passenger restraint system?

  1. 5 years
  2. 6 years
  3. 8 years
  4. 10 years
Show answer

C. 8 years

Every driver transporting a child under eight must use a child restraint system appropriate for the child's height and weight, with limited exceptions.

19. A new Georgia resident must get a Georgia license or permit within how many days?

  1. 90 days
  2. 10 days
  3. 30 days
  4. 60 days
Show answer

C. 30 days

New residents must obtain a Georgia driver's license or permit within 30 days of becoming a resident.

20. After a name or address change, a Georgia driver must update the license within how many days?

  1. 30 days
  2. 60 days
  3. 90 days
  4. 10 days
Show answer

B. 60 days

You must update your address or obtain a license with the correct name within 60 days.

21. Unless otherwise posted, what is the Georgia speed limit in an urban or residential district?

  1. 25 miles per hour
  2. 35 miles per hour
  3. 30 miles per hour
  4. 45 miles per hour
Show answer

C. 30 miles per hour

The limit is 30 mph in any urban or residential district unless posted otherwise.

22. Unless otherwise posted, what is the Georgia speed limit on a rural interstate?

  1. 75 miles per hour
  2. 55 miles per hour
  3. 65 miles per hour
  4. 70 miles per hour
Show answer

D. 70 miles per hour

It is 70 mph on a rural interstate and 65 mph on an urban interstate or multi-lane divided highway.

23. Unless otherwise posted, what is the Georgia speed limit on an unpaved county road?

  1. 45 miles per hour
  2. 25 miles per hour
  3. 35 miles per hour
  4. 55 miles per hour
Show answer

C. 35 miles per hour

The limit on an unpaved county road is 35 mph.

24. On a two-lane Georgia road, passing is prohibited within how many feet of a railroad crossing, bridge, viaduct or tunnel?

  1. 50 feet
  2. 500 feet
  3. 100 feet
  4. 200 feet
Show answer

C. 100 feet

Passing is prohibited within 100 feet of a railroad crossing and within 100 feet of a bridge, viaduct or tunnel.

25. In Georgia you may not park within how many feet of fire apparatus stopped in answer to a fire alarm?

  1. 50 feet
  2. 100 feet
  3. 500 feet
  4. 200 feet

Do not park within 500 feet of fire apparatus stopped in answer to an alarm.
