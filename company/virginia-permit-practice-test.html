Practice questions with answers

1. On part one of the Virginia knowledge exam, how many of the ten traffic sign questions must you answer correctly?

  1. Nine
  2. Seven
  3. Eight
  4. All ten
Show answer

D. All ten

You must answer all ten sign questions correctly before you can take part two.

2. What score do you need on part two (general knowledge) of the Virginia knowledge exam?

  1. At least 80 percent
  2. 100 percent
  3. At least 70 percent
  4. At least 75 percent
Show answer

A. At least 80 percent

Part two is multiple choice and requires at least 80 percent correct.

3. If you are under 18 and fail the Virginia knowledge exam, how long must you wait to retake it?

  1. 7 days
  2. A full 15 days
  3. 30 days
  4. One day
Show answer

B. A full 15 days

By law, a person under 18 who fails must wait a full 15 days.

4. A Virginia applicant age 18 or older must hold a learner's permit for how long before the first road skills test, unless they complete driver education?

  1. 90 days
  2. 60 days
  3. 30 days
  4. Nine months
Show answer

B. 60 days

Adults must hold the permit 60 days or complete an approved driver education course.

5. Unless posted otherwise, what is the Virginia maximum speed in school, business and residential areas?

  1. 20 MPH
  2. 35 MPH
  3. 30 MPH
  4. 25 MPH
Show answer

D. 25 MPH

The limit is 25 MPH for school, business and residential areas.

6. Unless posted otherwise, what is the Virginia maximum speed on unpaved roads?

  1. 45 MPH
  2. 25 MPH
  3. 55 MPH
  4. 35 MPH
Show answer

D. 35 MPH

Unpaved roads are 35 MPH and all other roads 55 MPH unless a sign says otherwise.

7. At speeds under 35 MPH on dry roads, what following distance does the Virginia manual give?

  1. 2 seconds
  2. 1 second
  3. 4 seconds
  4. 3 seconds
Show answer

A. 2 seconds

Virginia teaches 2 seconds under 35 MPH, 3 seconds at 35-45 MPH and 4 seconds at 46-70 MPH.

8. At 46 to 70 MPH on dry roads, what following distance does the Virginia manual give?

  1. 2 seconds
  2. 3 seconds
  3. 6 seconds
  4. 4 seconds
Show answer

D. 4 seconds

The space cushion is 4 seconds at 46-70 MPH. It does not work if you speed over 70.

9. In Virginia you should signal a turn how far ahead?

  1. 500 feet ahead
  2. Three or four seconds, 100 feet, ahead of the turn
  3. Only when other cars are near
  4. One second ahead
Show answer

B. Three or four seconds, 100 feet, ahead of the turn

Signal at least three or four seconds, 100 feet, before the turn.

10. In a Virginia shared center left-turn lane, you may not travel farther than

  1. 500 feet
  2. 150 feet
  3. 300 feet
  4. 50 feet
Show answer

B. 150 feet

Vehicles may use the lane to turn left but may not travel more than 150 feet in it.

11. In Virginia you may not park within how many feet of a fire hydrant?

  1. 30 feet
  2. 10 feet
  3. 20 feet
  4. 15 feet
Show answer

D. 15 feet

Parking is prohibited within 15 feet of a fire hydrant and within 15 feet of a fire or rescue station entrance.

12. In Virginia you may not park within how many feet of an intersection?

  1. 20 feet
  2. 50 feet
  3. 10 feet
  4. 15 feet
Show answer

A. 20 feet

Parking is prohibited within 20 feet of an intersection.

13. In Virginia you may not park within how many feet of a railroad crossing?

  1. 15 feet
  2. 50 feet
  3. 20 feet
  4. 100 feet
Show answer

B. 50 feet

Parking is prohibited within 50 feet of a railroad crossing.

14. In Virginia you may not park within, or follow closer than, how many feet of fire trucks answering an alarm?

  1. 500 feet
  2. 100 feet
  3. 200 feet
  4. 1,000 feet
Show answer

A. 500 feet

Do not park within 500 feet of fire equipment answering an alarm, and never follow an emergency vehicle closer than 500 feet when its lights are flashing.

15. Virginia law requires headlights in bad weather when visibility is reduced to

  1. One mile
  2. 100 feet
  3. 500 feet
  4. 1,000 feet
Show answer

C. 500 feet

Use headlights in rain, fog, snow or sleet when visibility is reduced to 500 feet, and whenever you use wipers because of bad weather.

16. In Virginia, a driver age 21 or older is considered to be driving under the influence at a BAC of

  1. .10 percent or higher
  2. .12 percent or higher
  3. .08 percent or higher
  4. .05 percent or higher
Show answer

C. .08 percent or higher

The limit is .08 percent. You can still be convicted below .08 if your driving is impaired.

17. In Virginia, a driver under 21 can be convicted of illegal consumption of alcohol at a BAC of at least

  1. .02
  2. .00
  3. .05
  4. .08
Show answer

A. .02

Under 21, a BAC of at least .02 but less than .08 is illegal consumption. At .08 or higher it may be a DUI.

18. In Virginia, when must you stop for a school bus with flashing red lights and an extended stop sign?

  1. Only on two-lane roads
  2. When approaching from any direction, unless a median or barrier separates you from the bus
  3. Only in school zones
  4. Only when you are behind the bus
Show answer

B. When approaching from any direction, unless a median or barrier separates you from the bus

Stop from any direction and stay stopped until everyone is clear and the bus moves. You need not stop if you travel in the opposite direction on a road with a median or barrier.

19. In Virginia, must you stop for a school bus that is loading passengers if its signals are not on?

  1. Only at night
  2. No
  3. Yes
  4. Only if children are in the road
Show answer

C. Yes

The manual says you must also stop if the bus is loading or unloading passengers and the signals are not on.

20. In Virginia, all children under what age must be secured in a child safety seat or booster seat?

  1. Under 6
  2. Under 8
  3. Under 12
  4. Under 5
Show answer

B. Under 8

All children under 8 must be properly secured in a child safety seat or booster seat.

21. In Virginia it is illegal to transport children under what age in the bed of a pickup truck?

  1. Under 12
  2. Under 8
  3. Under 16
  4. Under 18
Show answer

C. Under 16

Children under 16 may not ride in a pickup bed, even with a camper shell.

22. In Virginia, may you turn left at a red light?

  1. Yes, from a one-way street onto another one-way street after a complete stop
  2. Yes, without stopping
  3. Yes, from any street
  4. No, never
Show answer

A. Yes, from a one-way street onto another one-way street after a complete stop

A left on red is allowed only from one one-way street onto another, after a full stop and yielding.

23. In Virginia, your driving privilege is suspended for 90 days if you receive

  1. 12 demerit points in 24 months
  2. 18 demerit points in 12 months or 24 in 24 months
  3. 8 demerit points in 6 months
  4. 6 demerit points in 12 months
Show answer

B. 18 demerit points in 12 months or 24 in 24 months

At 18 points in 12 months or 24 in 24 months, DMV suspends for 90 days and requires a driver improvement clinic.

24. If you move, you must notify Virginia DMV of your new address within

  1. 30 days
  2. 10 days
  3. 60 days
  4. 90 days
Show answer

A. 30 days

Address changes must be reported within 30 days.

25. In Virginia, a driver entering a roundabout must

  1. Speed up to merge
  2. Stop even when it is empty
  3. Honk before entering
  4. Yield to traffic already in it
Show answer

D. Yield to traffic already in it

Drivers entering any intersection or roundabout must yield to traffic already in it.
