Practice questions with answers

1. Most of a motorcycle's stopping power comes from the

  1. Rear brake
  2. Front brake
  3. Clutch
  4. Engine
Show answer

B. Front brake

The front brake gives about 70% or more of your stopping power.

2. For the quickest safe stop, you should

  1. Use the rear brake only
  2. Pull in the clutch and coast
  3. Use both brakes at the same time
  4. Use the front brake only
Show answer

C. Use both brakes at the same time

Apply both smoothly and firmly without locking a wheel.

3. The single most important piece of protective gear is

  1. A bright jacket
  2. Leather gloves
  3. A helmet that meets DOT standards
  4. Riding boots
Show answer

C. A helmet that meets DOT standards

Head injuries are the main cause of rider deaths.

4. The best eye protection is

  1. A face shield
  2. Sunglasses
  3. A windshield alone
  4. Regular eyeglasses
Show answer

A. A face shield

A shield protects your whole face from wind, dust and insects.

5. To turn right at speed, you

  1. Press forward on the right handgrip
  2. Press forward on the left handgrip
  3. Turn the handlebars sharply right
  4. Lean your body only
Show answer

A. Press forward on the right handgrip

This is countersteering: press right, lean right, go right.

6. When approaching a curve, you should

  1. Brake hard in the middle of the turn
  2. Shift to neutral
  3. Speed up as you enter
  4. Slow down before the turn
Show answer

D. Slow down before the turn

Slow, look, press and roll on the throttle through the curve.

7. In a turn, you should look

  1. Through the turn to where you want to go
  2. At the outside edge
  3. At the road just ahead of the front wheel
  4. At your mirrors
Show answer

A. Through the turn to where you want to go

Turn your head, not just your eyes. The motorcycle goes where you look.

8. Most crashes between a car and a motorcycle happen

  1. On curves in the country
  2. On freeways
  3. In parking lots
  4. At intersections
Show answer

D. At intersections

The most common case is a car turning left in front of the rider.

9. To be seen by other drivers, you should

  1. Follow closely behind cars
  2. Ride in the right edge of the lane
  3. Wear bright clothing and keep your headlight on
  4. Ride only at night
Show answer

C. Wear bright clothing and keep your headlight on

Many drivers say they never saw the motorcycle.

10. Riding in a driver's blind spot is

  1. Dangerous, because the driver may change lanes into you
  2. The best place to be seen
  3. Required in heavy traffic
  4. Safe if you match their speed
Show answer

A. Dangerous, because the driver may change lanes into you

Move ahead or drop back so you appear in the mirrors.

11. A car and a motorcycle in the same lane

  1. Can share the lane side by side
  2. Is required on freeways
  3. Should share if traffic is slow
  4. Each need a full lane width to be safe
Show answer

D. Each need a full lane width to be safe

Do not let drivers squeeze beside you. Ride where you protect your lane.

12. The road is most slippery

  1. Once the rain stops
  2. Only when it snows
  3. After an hour of rain
  4. During the first few minutes of rain
Show answer

D. During the first few minutes of rain

Oil and dirt mix with the first water and make the surface slick.

13. On a slippery surface, you should

  1. Speed up to get across
  2. Brake hard with the front brake
  3. Lean more in turns
  4. Slow down and avoid sudden moves
Show answer

D. Slow down and avoid sudden moves

Keep the motorcycle as upright as you can.

14. You should cross railroad tracks that run across your path at an angle of

  1. Any angle at high speed
  2. Parallel to the tracks
  3. As close to 90 degrees as is safe, and at least 45
  4. Less than 20 degrees
Show answer

C. As close to 90 degrees as is safe, and at least 45

A shallow angle can catch your tire.

15. If you must ride over an object in the road, you should

  1. Lean far to one side
  2. Pull in the clutch and coast
  3. Rise slightly off the seat and keep a firm grip
  4. Brake hard as you hit it
Show answer

C. Rise slightly off the seat and keep a firm grip

Approach as straight as you can and roll on the throttle slightly just before contact.

16. If your front wheel locks while braking, you should

  1. Release the front brake at once, then reapply it smoothly
  2. Apply more rear brake
  3. Keep it locked until you stop
  4. Turn the handlebars
Show answer

A. Release the front brake at once, then reapply it smoothly

A locked front wheel will make you fall very quickly.

17. Your motorcycle starts to wobble. You should

  1. Accelerate out of it
  2. Fight it by steering back and forth
  3. Grip the bars firmly and close the throttle slowly
  4. Brake hard
Show answer

C. Grip the bars firmly and close the throttle slowly

Do not brake or speed up. Slow gradually, then pull off and check the load and tires.

18. If a tire goes flat while riding, you should

  1. Hold the grips firmly and ease off the throttle
  2. Brake hard on the flat tire
  3. Shift to a higher gear
  4. Swerve to the shoulder at once
Show answer

A. Hold the grips firmly and ease off the throttle

If you must brake, gently use the brake on the tire that is not flat.

19. If your throttle sticks, first

  1. Jump off the motorcycle
  2. Shift up a gear
  3. Twist it back and forth to free it
  4. Brake as hard as you can
Show answer

C. Twist it back and forth to free it

If that fails, use the engine cut-off switch and pull in the clutch together.

20. When you must swerve to avoid a hazard, you should

  1. Stand on the pegs
  2. Not brake while you are swerving
  3. Brake hard during the swerve
  4. Close your eyes and lean
Show answer

B. Not brake while you are swerving

Brake before or after the swerve, never during it.

21. A passenger should

  1. Lean with you and keep feet on the footrests
  2. Put feet down at stops
  3. Lean the opposite way in turns
  4. Sit as far back as possible
Show answer

A. Lean with you and keep feet on the footrests

Tell your passenger to hold on, sit still and keep feet up even when stopped.

22. Carrying a passenger makes the motorcycle

  1. Easier to balance at low speed
  2. Slower to stop, speed up and turn
  3. Handle the same
  4. Stop faster
Show answer

B. Slower to stop, speed up and turn

Ride slower and leave more space.

23. The best formation for a group on a straight road is

  1. Side by side in pairs
  2. Bunched close together
  3. Staggered
  4. Single file at all times
Show answer

C. Staggered

Staggering keeps the group close while giving each rider space.

24. At night you should

  1. Wear a tinted shield
  2. Ride closer to the car ahead to see better
  3. Slow down and leave more space ahead
  4. Use the low beam only
Show answer

C. Slow down and leave more space ahead

Use your high beam when no one is ahead or coming toward you.

25. A dog runs at your motorcycle. You should

  1. Swerve into the next lane
  2. Stop and wait
  3. Kick at the dog
  4. Slow down, then speed up as you get near it
Show answer

D. Slow down, then speed up as you get near it

Slowing and then accelerating throws off the dog's timing.

26. Alcohol first affects your

  1. Strength
  2. Hearing
  3. Vision only
  4. Judgment
Show answer

D. Judgment

Riding takes more skill than driving, and alcohol hits those skills early.

27. Before every ride you should check

  1. The paint
  2. Only the fuel level
  3. The seat height
  4. Tires, controls, lights, oil and chain
Show answer

D. Tires, controls, lights, oil and chain

A quick walk-around catches problems before they cause a crash.

28. For protection, besides a helmet you should wear

  1. A scarf and sneakers
  2. Shorts and sandals in summer
  3. A jacket, long pants, gloves and boots that cover the ankle
  4. Whatever is comfortable
Show answer

C. A jacket, long pants, gloves and boots that cover the ankle

Good gear protects your skin in a slide and helps drivers see you.

29. When parked at a curb, your motorcycle should be

  1. On the sidewalk
  2. At an angle with the rear wheel touching the curb
  3. Parallel, like a car
  4. With the front wheel against the curb
Show answer

B. At an angle with the rear wheel touching the curb

Parking at an angle lets you pull out safely.

30. When you slow to a stop, you should shift

  1. Down through the gears so you are in first
  2. Only after you have stopped
  3. Up one gear
  4. Into neutral early
Show answer

A. Down through the gears so you are in first

Staying in first lets you move off quickly if a vehicle comes up behind you.
