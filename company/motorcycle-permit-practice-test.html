Practice questions with answers

1. Most of a motorcycle's stopping power comes from the

  1. Rear brake
  2. Front brake
  3. Clutch
  4. Engine
Show answer

B. Front brake

The front brake gives about 70% or more of your stopping power.

2. For the quickest safe stop, you should

  1. Use the rear brake only
  2. Pull in the clutch and coast
  3. Use both brakes at the same time
  4. Use the front brake only
Show answer

C. Use both brakes at the same time

Apply both smoothly and firmly without locking a wheel.

3. The single most important piece of protective gear is

  1. A bright jacket
  2. Leather gloves
  3. A helmet that meets DOT standards
  4. Riding boots
Show answer

C. A helmet that meets DOT standards

Head injuries are the main cause of rider deaths.

4. The best eye protection is

  1. A face shield
  2. Sunglasses
  3. A windshield alone
  4. Regular eyeglasses
Show answer

A. A face shield

A shield protects your whole face from wind, dust and insects.

5. To turn right at speed, you

  1. Press forward on the right handgrip
  2. Press forward on the left handgrip
  3. Turn the handlebars sharply right
  4. Lean your body only
Show answer

A. Press forward on the right handgrip

This is countersteering: press right, lean right, go right.

6. When approaching a curve, you should

  1. Brake hard in the middle of the turn
  2. Shift to neutral
  3. Speed up as you enter
  4. Slow down before the turn
Show answer

D. Slow down before the turn

Slow, look, press and roll on the throttle through the curve.

7. In a turn, you should look

  1. Through the turn to where you want to go
  2. At the outside edge
  3. At the road just ahead of the front wheel
  4. At your mirrors
Show answer

A. Through the turn to where you want to go

Turn your head, not just your eyes. The motorcycle goes where you look.

8. Most crashes between a car and a motorcycle happen

  1. On curves in the country
  2. On freeways
  3. In parking lots
  4. At intersections
Show answer

D. At intersections

The most common case is a car turning left in front of the rider.

9. To be seen by other drivers, you should

  1. Follow closely behind cars
  2. Ride in the right edge of the lane
  3. Wear bright clothing and keep your headlight on
  4. Ride only at night
Show answer

C. Wear bright clothing and keep your headlight on

Many drivers say they never saw the motorcycle.

10. Riding in a driver's blind spot is

  1. Dangerous, because the driver may change lanes into you
  2. The best place to be seen
  3. Required in heavy traffic
  4. Safe if you match their speed
Show answer

A. Dangerous, because the driver may change lanes into you

Move ahead or drop back so you appear in the mirrors.

11. A car and a motorcycle in the same lane

  1. Can share the lane side by side
  2. Is required on freeways
  3. Should share if traffic is slow
  4. Each need a full lane width to be safe
Show answer

D. Each need a full lane width to be safe

Do not let drivers squeeze beside you. Ride where you protect your lane.

12. On a slippery surface, you should

  1. Speed up to get across
  2. Brake hard with the front brake
  3. Lean more in turns
  4. Slow down and avoid sudden moves
Show answer

D. Slow down and avoid sudden moves

Keep the motorcycle as upright as you can.

13. If your front wheel locks while braking, you should

  1. Release the front brake at once, then reapply it smoothly
  2. Apply more rear brake
  3. Keep it locked until you stop
  4. Turn the handlebars
Show answer

A. Release the front brake at once, then reapply it smoothly

A locked front wheel will make you fall very quickly.

14. A passenger should

  1. Lean with you and keep feet on the footrests
  2. Put feet down at stops
  3. Lean the opposite way in turns
  4. Sit as far back as possible
Show answer

A. Lean with you and keep feet on the footrests

Tell your passenger to hold on, sit still and keep feet up even when stopped.

15. Carrying a passenger makes the motorcycle

  1. Easier to balance at low speed
  2. Slower to stop, speed up and turn
  3. Handle the same
  4. Stop faster
Show answer

B. Slower to stop, speed up and turn

Ride slower and leave more space.

16. The best formation for a group on a straight road is

  1. Side by side in pairs
  2. Bunched close together
  3. Staggered
  4. Single file at all times
Show answer

C. Staggered

Staggering keeps the group close while giving each rider space.

17. At night you should

  1. Wear a tinted shield
  2. Ride closer to the car ahead to see better
  3. Slow down and leave more space ahead
  4. Use the low beam only
Show answer

C. Slow down and leave more space ahead

Use your high beam when no one is ahead or coming toward you.

18. Before every ride you should check

  1. The paint
  2. Only the fuel level
  3. The seat height
  4. Tires, controls, lights, oil and chain
Show answer

D. Tires, controls, lights, oil and chain

A quick walk-around catches problems before they cause a crash.

19. For protection, besides a helmet you should wear

  1. A scarf and sneakers
  2. Shorts and sandals in summer
  3. A jacket, long pants, gloves and boots that cover the ankle
  4. Whatever is comfortable
Show answer

C. A jacket, long pants, gloves and boots that cover the ankle

Good gear protects your skin in a slide and helps drivers see you.

20. A lane gives a motorcycle how many paths of travel?

  1. Two, left and right
  2. One, in the center
  3. Four
  4. Three: left, center and right
Show answer

D. Three: left, center and right

Think of the lane as three positions and pick the one where you are seen best and have room to move.

21. The best lane position is the one that

  1. Keeps you beside other vehicles
  2. Lets you be seen and gives you space to avoid hazards
  3. Is always the center
  4. Is always next to the curb
Show answer

B. Lets you be seen and gives you space to avoid hazards

No single position is always best. Change position as traffic and the road change.

22. The oily strip in the center of a lane is usually

  1. Wider than the tire tracks
  2. Too slippery to ever ride on
  3. The only safe place to ride
  4. Safe to ride on unless the road is wet
Show answer

D. Safe to ride on unless the road is wet

The center strip is usually no more than two feet wide. Avoid it in the first rain and at intersections where oil builds up.

23. At an intersection, the greatest danger to a rider is usually

  1. A parked car
  2. An oncoming car turning left across your path
  3. A car stopped behind you
  4. A car in the far lane going your way
Show answer

B. An oncoming car turning left across your path

Drivers turning left often fail to see a motorcycle. Slow down and be ready to stop or swerve.

24. Before changing lanes you should

  1. Check your mirrors and turn your head to check the blind spot
  2. Signal and move at once
  3. Check your mirrors only
  4. Sound your horn
Show answer

A. Check your mirrors and turn your head to check the blind spot

Motorcycle mirrors do not show everything. A head check is the only way to see a vehicle beside you.

25. Flashing your brake light before you slow down is most useful when

  1. Someone is following closely or you are slowing where others do not expect it
  2. You are parked
  3. You are speeding up
  4. You are riding alone on an empty road
Show answer

A. Someone is following closely or you are slowing where others do not expect it

A few light taps on the brake warn the driver behind you.

26. To stop quickly in a curve, the best method is to

  1. Straighten the motorcycle first if you can, then brake hard
  2. Shift to neutral
  3. Brake as hard as possible while leaned over
  4. Use only the rear brake
Show answer

A. Straighten the motorcycle first if you can, then brake hard

If you cannot straighten up, brake smoothly and increase pressure as the motorcycle straightens.

27. If you lock the rear wheel on a good surface, you should

  1. Shift up a gear
  2. Keep it locked until you have stopped
  3. Lean hard to one side
  4. Release it and stamp on it again
Show answer

B. Keep it locked until you have stopped

Releasing a locked rear wheel while the motorcycle is out of line can throw you off. Keep it locked and steer straight.

28. Rolling on the throttle slightly through a turn

  1. Should only be done on hills
  2. Helps keep the motorcycle stable
  3. Is dangerous on dry roads
  4. Makes the motorcycle fall inward
Show answer

B. Helps keep the motorcycle stable

Steady or gently increasing throttle settles the suspension through the curve.

29. In a slow, tight turn you should

  1. Stand on the footrests
  2. Brake hard with the front brake
  3. Lean your body far into the turn
  4. Keep your body upright and let the motorcycle lean
Show answer

D. Keep your body upright and let the motorcycle lean

In slow turns, counterbalance by keeping your body straight while the motorcycle leans.

30. A windshield on the motorcycle is

  1. Enough eye protection by itself
  2. A replacement for a helmet
  3. Only needed at night
  4. Not a substitute for a face shield or goggles
Show answer

D. Not a substitute for a face shield or goggles

Most windshields do not keep wind and debris out of your eyes.

31. A plastic face shield should be replaced when it is

  1. Clean
  2. Scratched enough to blur your vision
  3. One month old
  4. Tinted
Show answer

B. Scratched enough to blur your vision

Scratches scatter light and can blind you at night or in sun.

32. Which helmet gives the most protection?

  1. A half helmet
  2. A bicycle helmet
  3. A full-face helmet that meets DOT standards
  4. A cap worn under any helmet
Show answer

C. A full-face helmet that meets DOT standards

A full-face helmet protects the chin and face as well as the skull. It must fit snugly and be fastened.

33. Groups of riders should keep

  1. As large as possible
  2. Side by side in each lane
  3. Small, so they are easier to pass and less likely to be split up
  4. In single file at all times
Show answer

C. Small, so they are easier to pass and less likely to be split up

If a group is larger than four or five, split it into smaller groups.

34. Riding side by side with another motorcycle in one lane is

  1. The best way to be seen
  2. Unsafe, because neither rider has room to move
  3. Required in groups
  4. Safest at night
Show answer

B. Unsafe, because neither rider has room to move

Each rider needs the full lane to swerve or avoid a hazard.

35. A group should pass another vehicle

  1. One rider at a time
  2. All together
  3. On the shoulder
  4. Two at a time side by side
Show answer

A. One rider at a time

Each rider completes the pass and returns to position before the next one goes.

36. When riding at night, you should use your high beam

  1. Only in rain
  2. Only in town
  3. Never
  4. Whenever you are not following or meeting another vehicle
Show answer

D. Whenever you are not following or meeting another vehicle

High beams let you see farther. Dim them for oncoming traffic and when following.

37. To reduce fatigue on a long ride, you should

  1. Take a rest break at least every two hours and limit how far you go in a day
  2. Skip meals
  3. Ride faster to finish sooner
  4. Avoid wind protection
Show answer

A. Take a rest break at least every two hours and limit how far you go in a day

Wind, cold and vibration tire you faster on a motorcycle than in a car.

38. The front tire should be checked before each ride for

  1. Brand
  2. Air pressure, wear and damage
  3. Age only
  4. Color
Show answer

B. Air pressure, wear and damage

A tire with low pressure or damage can fail or handle badly.

39. What following distance does the Georgia motorcycle manual teach?

  1. At least four seconds
  2. One second
  3. Two car lengths
  4. Half a second
Show answer

A. At least four seconds

The manual uses a 4-second rule and says to open up more space when the road is slippery, when you cannot see through the vehicle ahead, or in heavy traffic.

40. When railroad tracks cross your lane at an angle, it is usually safer to

  1. Cross on the shoulder
  2. Swerve to cross at exactly 90 degrees
  3. Ride straight across within your lane
  4. Brake hard on the tracks
Show answer

C. Ride straight across within your lane

The manual warns that turning to take tracks at 90 degrees can be more dangerous, because your path may carry you into another lane of traffic.

41. For tracks and road seams that run parallel to your course, you should

  1. Ride along the top of the rail
  2. Move far enough away to cross them at an angle
  3. Cross them as slowly as you can at a shallow angle
  4. Ignore them
Show answer

B. Move far enough away to cross them at an angle

A tire can catch on an edge it meets at a shallow angle. Move away, then cross at a clear angle.

42. When riders in a group follow one another, they should keep a minimum following distance of

  1. Five seconds
  2. Half a second
  3. Two seconds
  4. One car length
Show answer

C. Two seconds

The manual's group riding rules say to keep a minimum two-second following distance and ride staggered.

43. If you need to stop while leaned over in a curve, you should

  1. Shift to neutral and coast
  2. Grab the front brake hard
  3. Use only the rear brake
  4. Apply smooth, gradual pressure to both brakes as you reduce your lean
Show answer

D. Apply smooth, gradual pressure to both brakes as you reduce your lean

The more you reduce the lean, the more traction you have for braking, so add brake pressure as the motorcycle straightens.

44. A passenger should keep both feet

  1. On the footrests, even when the motorcycle is stopped
  2. Dangling to help balance
  3. On the exhaust pipes
  4. On the ground at stops
Show answer

A. On the footrests, even when the motorcycle is stopped

The manual tells passengers to keep feet firmly on the footrests even when stopped and to keep legs away from mufflers, chains and moving parts.

45. Where should a passenger hold on?

  1. The handlebars
  2. Your shoulders only
  3. Your waist or hips, or the passenger handholds
  4. Nothing: holding on is unsafe
Show answer

C. Your waist or hips, or the passenger handholds

A firm hold on the rider's waist or hips, or the handholds, keeps the passenger stable when you brake or accelerate.

46. Before carrying a passenger or heavy load, you should check the owner's manual because

  1. Extra weight changes how the motorcycle handles and the suspension may need adjusting
  2. Passengers are illegal on most motorcycles
  3. The engine must be rebuilt
  4. It changes nothing
Show answer

A. Extra weight changes how the motorcycle handles and the suspension may need adjusting

The manual says extra weight affects operation and to adjust the motorcycle as the owner's manual directs.

47. Which statement about tinted eye protection is correct?

  1. It should not be worn at night or when there is little light
  2. It replaces a helmet
  3. It is required at all times
  4. It is best at night
Show answer

A. It should not be worn at night or when there is little light

Tinted shields and glasses cut the light you need to see in the dark.

48. Long-term exposure to wind noise on a motorcycle can

  1. Cause permanent hearing damage
  2. Only affect passengers
  3. Do nothing
  4. Improve your hearing
Show answer

A. Cause permanent hearing damage

Properly worn hearing protection reduces wind noise and still lets you hear horns and sirens.

49. Are jeans enough leg protection for riding?

  1. No. Jeans do not provide adequate protection
  2. Yes, if they are new
  3. Only in winter
  4. Yes, any jeans are enough
Show answer

A. No. Jeans do not provide adequate protection

The manual says sturdy synthetic or leather riding gear protects best and that jeans do not provide adequate protection.

50. In Georgia, a motorcycle rider must wear

  1. A helmet only on interstates
  2. A helmet that meets DOT standards
  3. Any hat with a chin strap
  4. A helmet only if under 21
Show answer

B. A helmet that meets DOT standards

The manual states that Georgia requires a DOT compliant helmet.

51. In Georgia, a motorcycle's headlight and taillight must be

  1. On only at night
  2. On only on highways
  3. On at all times
  4. On only in rain
Show answer

C. On at all times

The manual lists that the headlight and taillight must be illuminated at all times.

52. In Georgia, how far before a left turn may you enter the center turn lane?

  1. Any distance
  2. No more than 300 feet
  3. Up to half a mile
  4. No more than 100 feet
Show answer

B. No more than 300 feet

Georgia law limits the center lane to left turns, and you may enter it no more than 300 feet from the turn.

53. Can you be convicted of riding impaired in Georgia with a blood alcohol level under .08?

  1. Only if you are under 21
  2. Yes, if you are impaired
  3. No, never
  4. Only if you crash
Show answer

B. Yes, if you are impaired

The manual says that even under .08 you can be impaired and convicted of riding impaired.

54. A typical drink contains about how much alcohol?

  1. Two ounces
  2. None, if it is beer
  3. Half an ounce
  4. A full ounce and a half
Show answer

C. Half an ounce

A typical drink, such as one shot of spirits, has about half an ounce of alcohol.

55. If a driver is tailgating your motorcycle, the best choice is to

  1. Speed up to get away
  2. Brake hard to warn them
  3. Ignore them
  4. Change lanes and let them pass, or slow down to open more space ahead
Show answer

D. Change lanes and let them pass, or slow down to open more space ahead

Letting the tailgater by, or adding space ahead so you can stop gradually, removes the danger.

56. What do the letters in the riding strategy SEE stand for?

  1. Stop, Enter, Exit
  2. Search, Evaluate, Execute
  3. Speed, Energy, Escape
  4. Signal, Engage, Ease
Show answer

B. Search, Evaluate, Execute

Riders are taught to search for hazards, evaluate what could happen, and execute a response, always with an escape route.

57. An escape route is

  1. A route with no traffic lights
  2. An alternate path you can take if a hazard develops
  3. The lane behind you
  4. The shoulder of the road only
Show answer

B. An alternate path you can take if a hazard develops

The manual says to always use SEE and plan an escape route, whatever the conditions.

58. Successfully completing an approved Georgia motorcycle safety course can earn you

  1. A license test waiver
  2. An automatic helmet exemption
  3. Lower speed limits
  4. A commercial license
Show answer

A. A license test waiver

The Georgia Motorcycle Safety Program course provides training and a License Test Waiver when completed successfully.
