Practice questions with answers

1. What is the minimum age for an Arizona graduated instruction permit?

  1. 15 years and 6 months
  2. 14 years and 6 months
  3. 16 years
  4. 15 years
Show answer

A. 15 years and 6 months

You must be at least 15 years and 6 months old. The permit is valid for 12 months.

2. How long must an Arizona teen hold an instruction permit before applying for a graduated license?

  1. At least 30 days
  2. At least 3 months
  3. At least 12 months
  4. At least 6 months
Show answer

D. At least 6 months

An applicant for a graduated license must have held an Arizona instruction permit for at least 6 months.

3. How many hours of supervised practice does Arizona require for a graduated license without a driving course?

  1. 50 hours, including 10 at night
  2. 40 hours, including 10 at night
  3. 20 hours, including 5 at night
  4. 30 hours, including at least 10 at night
Show answer

D. 30 hours, including at least 10 at night

A parent or guardian certifies 30 hours with at least 10 at night, or 20 hours with 6 at night after an approved course.

4. For the first 6 months, an Arizona Class G license holder may not drive during which hours without an exception?

  1. Midnight to 5:00 a.m.
  2. 10 p.m. to 6 a.m.
  3. 1 a.m. to 4 a.m.
  4. 11 p.m. to 5 a.m.
Show answer

A. Midnight to 5:00 a.m.

No driving between midnight and 5 a.m. unless a licensed parent or guardian is in the front seat, or for school, work, religious activity or a family emergency.

5. For the first 6 months, how many passengers under 18 may an Arizona Class G license holder carry, other than siblings?

  1. No more than one
  2. No more than three
  3. None
  4. No limit
Show answer

A. No more than one

No more than one passenger under 18 unless they are siblings or a parent or guardian is in the front seat.

6. What does Arizona law say about wireless devices while driving?

  1. Texting is allowed at red lights
  2. Only teens are restricted
  3. You may not hold or support a device, but hands-free use is allowed
  4. Hand-held calls are allowed
Show answer

C. You may not hold or support a device, but hands-free use is allowed

Arizona prohibits holding or supporting a wireless device while driving. Hands-free and voice-to-text are allowed.

7. Where no limit is posted, what is the Arizona speed limit approaching a school crosswalk?

  1. 10 mph
  2. 25 mph
  3. 15 mph
  4. 20 mph
Show answer

C. 15 mph

The limit is 15 mph approaching a school crosswalk.

8. Where no limit is posted, what is the Arizona speed limit in a business or residential district?

  1. 35 mph
  2. 20 mph
  3. 30 mph
  4. 25 mph
Show answer

D. 25 mph

Business and residential districts are 25 mph.

9. Where no limit is posted, what is the Arizona speed limit on rural freeways?

  1. 80 mph
  2. 75 mph
  3. 70 mph
  4. 65 mph
Show answer

B. 75 mph

The manual lists 55 mph on open highways and city freeways, 65 mph on designated open highways and 75 mph on rural freeways.

10. In Arizona you should signal at least how far before turning?

  1. 50 feet
  2. 200 feet
  3. 300 feet
  4. 100 feet
Show answer

D. 100 feet

Signal at least 100 feet, about 4 seconds, before turning.

11. In Arizona you may not park within how many feet of a fire hydrant?

  1. 10 feet
  2. 30 feet
  3. 15 feet
  4. 20 feet
Show answer

C. 15 feet

Parking is prohibited within 15 feet of a fire hydrant.

12. In Arizona you may not park within how many feet of a crosswalk at an intersection?

  1. 15 feet
  2. 10 feet
  3. 20 feet
  4. 50 feet
Show answer

C. 20 feet

Parking is prohibited within 20 feet of a crosswalk at an intersection.

13. In Arizona you may not park within how many feet of a railroad crossing?

  1. 20 feet
  2. 100 feet
  3. 15 feet
  4. 50 feet
Show answer

D. 50 feet

Parking is prohibited within 50 feet of a railroad crossing.

14. What following distance does the Arizona manual teach?

  1. One car length
  2. Exactly 2 seconds
  3. 1 second
  4. At least 3 seconds, and 3 to 6 seconds in poor conditions
Show answer

D. At least 3 seconds, and 3 to 6 seconds in poor conditions

Arizona teaches the 3-6 second rule: at least 3 seconds normally, more on slippery roads.

15. In Arizona, when must you stop for a school bus that is loading or unloading?

  1. Only when you are behind the bus
  2. Only when children are in the road
  3. From either direction, unless you are on the opposite side of a divided roadway
  4. Only in school zones
Show answer

C. From either direction, unless you are on the opposite side of a divided roadway

Stop before reaching the bus regardless of direction and stay stopped until it moves or the stop arm and lights are off. Opposite traffic on a divided roadway need not stop.

16. In Arizona, how far must you stay behind a fire vehicle responding to an emergency?

  1. At least 200 feet
  2. At least 1,000 feet
  3. At least 500 feet
  4. At least 100 feet
Show answer

C. At least 500 feet

Stay at least 500 feet behind a responding fire vehicle.

17. In Arizona, children under what age must ride in a child restraint system?

  1. Under 2
  2. Under 5
  3. Under 3
  4. Under 4
Show answer

B. Under 5

Children under 5 must be in a child restraint. Children 5 to 8 stay in one until they are at least 4 feet 9 inches tall.

18. When passing a bicycle in your lane in Arizona, allow at least

  1. 1 foot of clearance
  2. 2 feet of clearance
  3. 3 feet of clearance
  4. 6 feet of clearance
Show answer

C. 3 feet of clearance

Arizona requires at least 3 feet of clearance.

19. You must notify Arizona MVD of a change of name or address within

  1. 90 days
  2. 60 days
  3. 10 days
  4. 30 days
Show answer

C. 10 days

Changes must be reported within 10 days.

20. In Arizona, a left turn on a red light is allowed

  1. From any road
  2. Only from a one-way road onto a one-way road, after stopping and yielding
  3. Never
  4. Only with a police officer present
Show answer

B. Only from a one-way road onto a one-way road, after stopping and yielding

Left on red is allowed only one-way to one-way unless signs prohibit it.

21. In Arizona, you lose your driving privilege on the spot if a test shows a BAC of

  1. 0.12 percent or more
  2. 0.05 percent or more
  3. 0.10 percent or more
  4. 0.08 percent or more
Show answer

D. 0.08 percent or more

The limit is 0.08 percent, and 0.04 in a commercial vehicle.

22. What is "Extreme DUI" in Arizona?

  1. Any second DUI
  2. A BAC of 0.15 or higher
  3. A BAC of 0.10 or higher
  4. A BAC of 0.08 or higher
Show answer

B. A BAC of 0.15 or higher

Extreme DUI is 0.15 or higher and carries at least 30 days in jail for a first offense.

23. What does Arizona's Zero Tolerance rule mean for drivers under 21?

  1. Only a warning is given
  2. A BAC under 0.08 is allowed
  3. A BAC under 0.05 is allowed
  4. Any alcohol concentration can bring a 2-year license suspension
Show answer

D. Any alcohol concentration can bring a 2-year license suspension

Under 21, any alcohol concentration can lead to suspension. The manual lists a 2-year suspension.

24. In Arizona, refusing a chemical test after a DUI arrest means losing your driving privilege for

  1. 12 months
  2. 6 months
  3. 90 days
  4. 30 days
Show answer

A. 12 months

A refusal brings an automatic 12-month loss, or 24 months for a second refusal within 84 months.

25. On an Arizona highway, switch from high beams to low beams when an oncoming vehicle is within

  1. 200 feet
  2. 500 feet
  3. 100 feet
  4. 1,000 feet
Show answer

B. 500 feet

Use high beams only when no vehicle is coming within 500 feet, and dim when following within 200 feet.

26. What happens in Arizona if you get 8 or more points within 12 months?

  1. Your license is revoked for life
  2. You pay a fee only
  3. You must attend Traffic Survival School or your license may be suspended
  4. Nothing
Show answer

C. You must attend Traffic Survival School or your license may be suspended

Eight or more points in any 12-month period requires Traffic Survival School, or the driving privilege may be suspended.

27. In Arizona, children between five and eight must ride in a child restraint system until they are at least

  1. 5 feet tall
  2. 4 feet tall
  3. 57 pounds
  4. 4 feet 9 inches tall
Show answer

D. 4 feet 9 inches tall

Children between five and eight years of age are required to be in a child restraint system until they are a minimum of four feet, nine inches tall.
