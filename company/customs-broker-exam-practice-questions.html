Free Customs Broker Exam Practice Questions (CBLE) with Official Answers

The Customs Broker License Examination is given by U.S. Customs and Border Protection twice a year, in April and October. It has 80 questions, lasts 4.5 hours, is open book, and the passing score is 75%. The next exam is April 28, 2027. You must be a U.S. citizen and at least 21 to be licensed; no experience is required.

Below are 30 free practice questions. Pick your answer, then tap Show answer.

1. What is the license application fee for an individual?

  1. $200
  2. $390
  3. $300
  4. $500
Show answer

C. $300

19 CFR 111.96(a): $300 for an individual, $500 for a partnership, association or corporation. $390 is the exam fee.

2. Customs Broker Jones receives an electronic notification via ABI Message UC2 from CBP requesting documents for an entry submitted that same day. How must Broker Jones respond?

  1. Contact CBP to request that an official CBP Form 28, Request for Information, be issued specifying the documents requested.
  2. Produce the records by the time of entry summary filing.
  3. Wait for CBP to specify the due date before producing the records.
  4. Produce the records within 30 calendar days of the receipt of demand.
Show answer

B. Produce the records by the time of entry summary filing.

Official answer: Produce the records by the time of entry summary filing. Look it up: 19 CFR 142.3; ACE BRPD Section 1.13. (CBP October 2026 exam, question 14.)

3. Your firm finds that a client undervalued goods on several past entries. What must the broker do?

  1. Nothing, because the entries were the client's responsibility
  2. Report the client to CBP within 24 hours
  3. Correct the entries without telling the client
  4. Advise the client of the error and how to correct it
Show answer

D. Advise the client of the error and how to correct it

19 CFR 111.39(b): a broker that knows of a client's noncompliance, error or omission must advise the client and the proper corrective action.

4. Which answer choice correctly completes the following statement: Upon CBP's request, the individual broker must make available to CBP the information and documentation pertaining to the qualifying continuing broker education completed during a triennial period on or before _____.

  1. 30 business days from the date of CBP's request
  2. 30 business days from the date of receipt of CBP's request
  3. 30 calendar days from the date of CBP's request
  4. 30 calendar days from the date of receipt of CBP's request
Show answer

D. 30 calendar days from the date of receipt of CBP's request

Official answer: 30 calendar days from the date of receipt of CBP's request Look it up: 19 CFR 111.102(d)(2). (CBP October 2025 exam, question 17.)

5. When must customs brokers notify CBP of a change in address for their office of record?

6. What are the requirements for CBP to consider an offer in compromise under the Tariff Act of 1930 (as amended) arising from a violation of Customs law?

B. Tender of the specific sum offered and an offer in writing.

Official answer: Tender of the specific sum offered and an offer in writing. Look it up: 19 CFR 161.5. (CBP October 2025 exam, question 4.)

  1. Switchblade knife components do not qualify as a switchblade knife.

B. Importation of a switchblade knife with a 2-inch blade is permitted when transported in the possession of an amputee with only one arm.

Official answer: Importation of a switchblade knife with a 2-inch blade is permitted when transported in the possession of an amputee with only one arm. Look it up: 19 CFR 12.95 - 12.98. (CBP October 2025 exam, question 42.)

11. CBP liquidated an entry, ascertaining the duties, fees, and taxes at $750.00. However, the estimated duties, fees, and taxes the broker had deposited at entry amounted to $1,000.00. What is legally required to occur to address the discrepancy between the estimated duties and ascertained duties?

  1. CBP should refund the broker $250.00 with interest.
  2. The broker must withdraw the entry and make another deposit to correct amount of duties, fees, and taxes.
  3. The broker must file a protest to request a refund.
  4. CBP should disregard the difference because it less than $300.00, a de minimus amount.
Show answer

A. CBP should refund the broker $250.00 with interest.

Official answer: CBP should refund the broker $250.00 with interest. Look it up: 19 CFR 159.6(c). (CBP April 2025 exam, question 45.)

12. Goods were entered under a TIB. What is the longest total period they can stay with all extensions?

  1. 5 years
  2. 2 years
  3. 1 year
  4. 3 years
Show answer

D. 3 years

19 CFR 10.37: the 1-year TIB period may be extended, but not beyond 3 years total from the date of importation.

13. Which of these is legally binding for classification?

  1. The Explanatory Notes
  2. Prior entries of the same goods
  3. CBP's Informed Compliance Publications
  4. Section and chapter notes
Show answer

D. Section and chapter notes

Section and chapter notes are part of the HTSUS and legally binding (GRI 1). The Explanatory Notes are persuasive guidance only.

16. Two headings both describe the goods. Under GRI 3(a) the importer uses

  1. The heading with the most specific description
  2. The heading the importer used last year
  3. The heading with the lowest duty rate
  4. The heading that comes first in the tariff
Show answer

A. The heading with the most specific description

GRI 3(a): the most specific description is preferred over a more general one.

17. Which GRI covers an unassembled bicycle shipped as a kit with all parts?

  1. GRI 3(b)
  2. GRI 1 only
  3. GRI 5(a)
  4. GRI 2(a)
Show answer

D. GRI 2(a)

GRI 2(a) extends a heading to articles imported unassembled or disassembled when they have the essential character of the finished article.

20. Royalties paid to the seller as a condition of the sale are

  1. Deducted from value
  2. Treated as buying commissions
  3. Never dutiable
  4. Added to transaction value
Show answer

D. Added to transaction value

Royalties are dutiable when the buyer must pay them as a condition of the sale.

21. Design work done in the United States and given free to the foreign maker is

  1. Treated as a selling commission
  2. Not an assist
  3. Always a dutiable assist
  4. Added at twice its cost
Show answer

B. Not an assist

Engineering, design and plans count as assists only if done outside the United States.

25. Most imported motor vehicles must meet the safety standards of

27. Imported pesticides need a notice of arrival filed with

  1. FINRA
  2. The FDA
  3. The EPA
  4. The USDA's Forest Service
Show answer

C. The EPA

EPA regulates pesticide imports under FIFRA.

28. An absolute quota fills on day 3 of the quota period. What happens to further imports?

  1. They enter right away, but at a higher over-quota duty rate
  2. They cannot be entered for consumption until the next period
  3. They enter duty-free
  4. They are seized and forfeited
Show answer

B. They cannot be entered for consumption until the next period

Absolute quotas cap the quantity; above the cap goods can be exported or held, e.g. in a bonded warehouse or FTZ, until the next period. A tariff-rate quota would charge a higher rate instead.

29. Imported food is regulated, in addition to CBP, mainly by

  2. The FDA
  3. The Department of Education
  4. The SEC
Show answer

B. The FDA

FDA requires prior notice for imported food shipments.

FCC rules cover radio-frequency devices.
