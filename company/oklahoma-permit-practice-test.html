Practice questions with answers

1. How long must an Oklahoma teen hold a learner permit before an intermediate license?

  1. At least one year
  2. At least 30 days
  3. At least 180 days
  4. At least 90 days
Show answer

C. At least 180 days

The learner permit must be held at least 180 days.

2. In Oklahoma, your license is revoked if a test shows a BAC of

  1. 0.08 or more, or any measurable amount if you are under 21
  2. 0.08 or more for everyone
  3. 0.10 or more for everyone
  4. 0.05 or more for everyone
Show answer

A. 0.08 or more, or any measurable amount if you are under 21

At 0.08, or any measurable amount under 21, the license is revoked even without a court conviction.

3. On an Oklahoma divided highway, must approaching traffic stop for a school bus on the other side?

  1. No
  2. Yes, at night
  3. Yes, always
  4. Yes, in cities
Show answer

A. No

Approaching traffic is not required to stop for a school bus on a divided highway.

4. What is the Oklahoma penalty for failing to stop for a school bus with red loading lights?

  1. Mandatory license revocation for one year
  2. Two points
  3. A warning
  4. A $25 fine only
Show answer

A. Mandatory license revocation for one year

The manual lists mandatory revocation of the driver license for one year.

5. When leaving an Oklahoma highway, signal right at least how far before the exit?

  1. 100 feet
  2. 500 feet
  3. 50 feet
  4. 200 feet
Show answer

A. 100 feet

Signal at least 100 feet before the exit.

6. In Oklahoma you may not park within how many feet of a fire hydrant?

  1. 5 feet
  2. 15 feet
  3. 10 feet
  4. 25 feet
Show answer

B. 15 feet

Parking is not allowed within 15 feet of a fire hydrant.

7. In Oklahoma you may not park within how many feet of the nearest rail of a railroad crossing?

  1. 30 feet
  2. 15 feet
  3. 100 feet
  4. 50 feet
Show answer

D. 50 feet

The limit is 50 feet.

8. In Oklahoma you may not park within how many feet of a fire station driveway entrance?

  1. 50 feet
  2. 30 feet
  3. 10 feet
  4. 20 feet
Show answer

D. 20 feet

The limit is 20 feet.

9. In Oklahoma, dim your high beams within how far of an oncoming vehicle?

  1. 1,000 feet
  2. 200 feet
  3. 500 feet
  4. 300 feet
Show answer

A. 1,000 feet

Dim within 1,000 feet of an oncoming vehicle and within 600 feet of a vehicle you are following.

10. In Oklahoma, dim your high beams within how far of a vehicle you are following?

  1. 300 feet
  2. 200 feet
  3. 600 feet
  4. 100 feet
Show answer

C. 600 feet

The following distance for dimming is 600 feet.

11. What following distance does the Oklahoma manual teach?

  1. 10 seconds
  2. 1 second
  3. 3 seconds, and 4 seconds in bad weather
  4. 2 seconds in all weather
Show answer

C. 3 seconds, and 4 seconds in bad weather

Stay 3 seconds behind the vehicle in front and allow 4 seconds in bad weather.

12. When passing a bicycle in Oklahoma, leave a distance of not less than

  1. 6 feet
  2. 2 feet
  3. 3 feet
  4. 1 foot
Show answer

C. 3 feet

Leave at least 3 feet until the motor vehicle is safely past.

13. In Oklahoma, your license is suspended if you accumulate

  1. 8 points within 2 years
  2. 10 or more points within 5 years
  3. 6 points within 1 year
  4. 12 points within 3 years
Show answer

B. 10 or more points within 5 years

Ten or more points within 5 years brings a suspension.

14. In Oklahoma, a vehicle must stop how far from a blind person in the roadway?

  1. 5 feet
  2. 15 feet
  3. 25 feet
  4. 10 feet
Show answer

B. 15 feet

A vehicle must stop 15 feet from a blind person in the roadway.

15. How old must you be, and how long must you have had your Oklahoma permit, to take the driving test?

  1. 16, with no holding period
  2. 16, with the permit held at least 6 months
  3. 15, with the permit held 30 days
  4. 17, with the permit held 1 year
Show answer

B. 16, with the permit held at least 6 months

You must be 16 years old and have had your permit for at least 6 months to take the driving test.

16. Between which hours may an Oklahoma intermediate (graduated) driver drive without exceptions?

  1. Any time
  2. 6 AM to midnight
  3. 5 AM to 11 PM
  4. 5 AM to 10 PM
Show answer

D. 5 AM to 10 PM

Driving is allowed from 5 AM to 10 PM unless for school, church or work activities, or with a licensed driver at least 21.

17. How many passengers may an Oklahoma intermediate driver carry without a licensed adult 21+ in front?

  1. Two
  2. One, unless all passengers live in the driver's household
  3. None
  4. Three
Show answer

B. One, unless all passengers live in the driver's household

Teens with a restricted license are limited to one passenger unless all passengers live in the same household.

18. In Oklahoma you may not park within how many feet of a crosswalk at an intersection?

  1. 20 feet
  2. 15 feet
  3. 30 feet
  4. 10 feet
Show answer

A. 20 feet

Do not park within 20 feet of a crosswalk at an intersection.

19. In Oklahoma you may not park within how many feet of a flashing beacon, stop sign or traffic signal at the side of the road?

  1. 15 feet
  2. 50 feet
  3. 20 feet
  4. 30 feet
Show answer

D. 30 feet

Do not park within 30 feet of a flashing beacon, stop sign or traffic control signal at the side of the roadway.

20. Which Oklahoma children must ride in a child passenger restraint system?

  1. Children under 12
  2. Children under 2 only
  3. Children under 40 pounds only
  4. Children under 8, unless taller than 4 feet 9 inches once they are 4
Show answer

D. Children under 8, unless taller than 4 feet 9 inches once they are 4

Drivers must restrain children under 8; a child 4 to 7 who is not taller than 4 feet 9 inches must use a child restraint.

21. Which vehicles are required by Oklahoma law to stop at railroad crossings?

  1. All trucks
  2. Motorcycles
  3. Only vehicles over 26,000 pounds
  4. School buses, church buses, motor coaches and vehicles hauling explosives or flammable liquids
Show answer

D. School buses, church buses, motor coaches and vehicles hauling explosives or flammable liquids

These vehicles must stop at crossings, so do not pass a vehicle that is required to stop.

22. What does Oklahoma's Move Over law require on a multi-lane road when a stopped vehicle has flashing lights?

  1. Stop behind it
  2. Nothing unless it is police
  3. Move to a lane not next to it if safe; otherwise slow down
  4. Honk and pass
Show answer

C. Move to a lane not next to it if safe; otherwise slow down

Move over to a lane away from the stopped vehicle if safe; if not, slow down and drive carefully.

23. How many points are removed from an Oklahoma driving record for each 12 months with no convictions?

  1. All of them
  2. Two
  3. One
  4. Five
Show answer

B. Two

Two points are deducted for each 12-month period with no pointable traffic convictions.
