Practice questions with answers

1. What score do you need to pass the Kansas knowledge test?

  1. 90%
  2. 80%
  3. 70%
  4. 75%
Show answer

B. 80%

You must score 80% to pass the knowledge test.

2. What is the minimum age for a Kansas Class C instruction permit?

  1. 16
  2. 15
  3. 14
  4. 15 and a half
Show answer

C. 14

A Class C or M instruction permit is available at 14 or older.

3. How many hours of practice driving does Kansas require for teen licensing?

  1. 50 hours, with 10 at night
  2. 20 hours, with 5 at night
  3. 40 hours, with 6 at night
  4. 30 hours, with 10 at night
Show answer

A. 50 hours, with 10 at night

Kansas requires 50 hours of practice with an adult 21 or older, 10 of those at night.

4. In Kansas, driving is unlawful with a test result of

  1. .05 or above
  2. .10 or above
  3. .12 or above
  4. .08 or above
Show answer

D. .08 or above

Testing .08 or above is driving under the influence.

5. In Kansas, a driver under 21 can be arrested for alcohol impairment at

  1. .02%
  2. .00%
  3. .08%
  4. .05%
Show answer

A. .02%

Under 21, the level is .02%.

6. In Kansas, when must you stop for a stopped school bus?

  1. Only in school zones
  2. Only when behind it
  3. Only if children are in the road
  4. When meeting or overtaking it while it picks up or lets off children
Show answer

D. When meeting or overtaking it while it picks up or lets off children

Stop when meeting or overtaking a school, church or day care bus and stay stopped until the stop signal is retracted and the red lights are off.

7. On a Kansas divided highway, must traffic on the opposing roadway stop for a school bus?

  1. Yes, always
  2. Yes, in cities
  3. No
  4. Yes, at night
Show answer

C. No

Traffic approaching a school bus on the opposing roadway of a divided highway is not required to stop.

8. In Kansas, all turn signals must be given at least how far before the move or turn?

  1. 300 feet
  2. 100 feet
  3. 50 feet
  4. 200 feet
Show answer

B. 100 feet

Signals must be given at least 100 feet before the actual move or turn.

9. In Kansas you may not park within how many feet of a fire hydrant?

  1. 15 feet
  2. 10 feet
  3. 5 feet
  4. 25 feet
Show answer

A. 15 feet

Parking is not allowed within 15 feet of a fire hydrant.

10. In Kansas you may not park within how many feet of the nearest rail at a railroad crossing?

  1. 50 feet
  2. 100 feet
  3. 30 feet
  4. 15 feet
Show answer

A. 50 feet

The limit is 50 feet.

11. In Kansas you may not park within how many feet of a fire station driveway entrance?

  1. 10 feet
  2. 20 feet
  3. 50 feet
  4. 30 feet
Show answer

B. 20 feet

The limit is 20 feet.

12. When parked at a Kansas curb, your vehicle may be no more than how far from it?

  1. 6 inches
  2. 12 inches
  3. 24 inches
  4. 18 inches
Show answer

B. 12 inches

Parking more than 12 inches from the curb is not allowed.

13. What following distance does the Kansas handbook teach under prime conditions?

  1. The four-second rule
  2. One car length
  3. The one-second rule
  4. The two-second rule
Show answer

D. The two-second rule

Use two seconds in prime conditions and four seconds in adverse conditions.

14. In Kansas, all children under what age must be restrained in a federally approved child safety seat?

  1. Under 2
  2. Under 8
  3. Under 4
  4. Under 6
Show answer

C. Under 4

Children under 4 need a child safety seat. Children 4 to 8 need a safety seat or booster.

15. Unless otherwise posted, what is the Kansas maximum speed in a properly marked school zone or business district?

  1. 30 mph
  2. 25 mph
  3. 20 mph
  4. 15 mph
Show answer

C. 20 mph

The listed maximum is 20 mph in marked school zones and business districts.
