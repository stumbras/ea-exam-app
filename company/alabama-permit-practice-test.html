Practice questions with answers

1. A school bus is stopped on a two-lane road with red lights flashing and its stop arm out. You must

  1. Honk and proceed
  2. Stop, no matter which direction you are going
  3. Stop only if you are behind the bus
  4. Pass slowly on the left
Show answer

B. Stop, no matter which direction you are going

Children may cross from either side. Wait until the lights stop and the arm is pulled in.

2. At a stop sign with no stop line or crosswalk, stop

  1. Only if other cars are present
  2. In the middle of the intersection
  3. Before entering the intersection, where you can see cross traffic
  4. Ten car lengths back
Show answer

C. Before entering the intersection, where you can see cross traffic

Stop at the stop line first, then the crosswalk, then the edge of the intersection.

3. In slow traffic, you should cross railroad tracks only when

  1. The lights begin to flash
  2. There is room for your car on the other side
  3. You are in a hurry
  4. The car ahead has started across
Show answer

B. There is room for your car on the other side

Never stop on the tracks.

4. When backing up, you should

  1. Open the door to look
  2. Turn and look through the rear window
  3. Back up quickly
  4. Use only the mirrors
Show answer

B. Turn and look through the rear window

Back slowly and check all around the car first.

5. At a railroad crossing with red lights flashing, you must

  1. Slow down and cross if no train is seen
  2. Speed up to cross
  3. Drive around the gates
  4. Stop and wait until the lights stop
Show answer

D. Stop and wait until the lights stop

A second train may be coming.

6. A flashing yellow light means

  1. Stop and wait for green
  2. Speed up to clear the intersection
  3. The light is broken, so ignore it
  4. Slow down and proceed with caution
Show answer

D. Slow down and proceed with caution

You do not have to stop, but be ready to.

7. You miss your freeway exit. You should

  1. Stop and wait for a gap to cross
  2. Make a U-turn across the median
  3. Back up on the shoulder
  4. Go on to the next exit
Show answer

D. Go on to the next exit

Never back up or stop on a freeway.

8. A yellow, diamond-shaped sign is a

  1. Guide sign
  2. Warning sign
  3. Service sign
  4. Regulatory sign
Show answer

B. Warning sign

Diamond signs warn of hazards or changes in the road ahead.

9. You have a green light and want to turn left. There is no arrow. You must

  1. Turn first because you have the green
  2. Stop and wait for an arrow
  3. Wait for a red light
  4. Yield to oncoming traffic and pedestrians
Show answer

D. Yield to oncoming traffic and pedestrians

A plain green light does not protect a left turn.

10. A red, eight-sided sign means

  1. Stop completely
  2. Slow down and proceed
  3. Do not enter
  4. Yield to other traffic
Show answer

A. Stop completely

The octagon shape is used only for stop signs. Come to a full stop.

11. A flashing red light means

  1. Slow down and proceed
  2. Stop, then go when it is safe
  3. The signal is about to turn green
  4. Stop only for pedestrians
Show answer

B. Stop, then go when it is safe

Treat a flashing red light like a stop sign.

12. Your rear wheels start to skid. You should

  1. Take your foot off the gas and steer where you want to go
  2. Steer the opposite way
  3. Accelerate
  4. Brake hard
Show answer

A. Take your foot off the gas and steer where you want to go

Look and steer in the direction you want the front of the car to go.

13. When entering a roundabout, you must

  1. Yield to traffic entering behind you
  2. Stop even if it is empty
  3. Yield to traffic already in the circle
  4. Enter at full speed
Show answer

C. Yield to traffic already in the circle

Traffic moves counterclockwise. Enter when there is a gap.

14. Two cars reach a four-way stop at the same time. Who goes first?

  1. The larger vehicle
  2. The driver going straight
  3. The driver on the left
  4. The driver on the right
Show answer

D. The driver on the right

If you arrive together, the driver on the left yields to the driver on the right.

15. You feel sleepy while driving. The best thing to do is

  1. Drive faster to get home
  2. Pull off the road and rest
  3. Turn up the radio
  4. Open the window
Show answer

B. Pull off the road and rest

Nothing replaces sleep. Drowsy driving causes crashes.

16. Your brake pedal goes to the floor. First you should

  1. Pump the brake pedal
  2. Shift to reverse
  3. Turn off the engine
  4. Open the door
Show answer

A. Pump the brake pedal

If pumping fails, shift to a lower gear and use the parking brake gently.

17. What lowers the alcohol level in your body?

  1. Time
  2. A cold shower
  3. Fresh air
  4. Coffee
Show answer

A. Time

Only time lets your body remove alcohol.

18. The speed limit is 55 mph, but there is heavy fog. You should

  1. Drive exactly 55 mph
  2. Follow the car ahead closely
  3. Drive slower than 55, at a speed safe for conditions
  4. Drive faster to get out of the fog
Show answer

C. Drive slower than 55, at a speed safe for conditions

You must never drive faster than is safe for conditions.

19. You come to an intersection where the traffic lights are dark. You should

  1. Wait for the lights to come back
  2. Drive through without slowing
  3. Treat it as an all-way stop
  4. Follow the car ahead
Show answer

C. Treat it as an all-way stop

Stop, then take turns as at a four-way stop.

20. Orange signs warn drivers about

  1. School crossings
  2. Construction and work zones
  3. Recreation areas
  4. Hospitals and services
Show answer

B. Construction and work zones

Orange means road work. Slow down and watch for workers.

21. When merging onto a freeway, you should

  1. Merge without looking
  2. Match the speed of traffic and merge into a gap
  3. Stop at the end of the ramp
  4. Drive slowly until a driver lets you in
Show answer

B. Match the speed of traffic and merge into a gap

Use the ramp to build speed, signal and yield to freeway traffic.

22. Your car starts to hydroplane on a wet road. You should

  1. Brake hard
  2. Turn the wheel sharply
  3. Speed up
  4. Ease off the gas and steer straight
Show answer

D. Ease off the gas and steer straight

Let the car slow until the tires grip again.

23. A white rectangular sign with black letters usually

  1. Gives directions to services
  2. Marks a work zone
  3. Warns of a hazard ahead
  4. States a rule you must obey, such as a speed limit
Show answer

D. States a rule you must obey, such as a speed limit

White rectangles are regulatory signs.

24. Seat belts should be worn

  1. Only on highways
  2. Only in the front seat
  3. By the driver and all passengers on every trip
  4. Only in bad weather
Show answer

C. By the driver and all passengers on every trip

Seat belts are the best protection in a crash.

25. Two solid yellow lines in the center of the road mean

  1. No passing from either direction
  2. A bike lane
  3. A one-way road
  4. Passing is allowed for both directions
Show answer

A. No passing from either direction

You may cross them only to turn left into a driveway or road where allowed.
