Questions people ask

Is it really free?

Yes. The citizenship, driver's license and motorcycle tests are free with no card and no trial. You can sign in to save your progress, but you do not have to.

Where do the questions come from?

The citizenship questions are the official USCIS civics list. Driving and motorcycle questions are written from each state's official driver manual, and the app names the manual and edition it used.

Which states are covered?

Road signs and rules of the road apply in every state. 50 states also have their own questions on speed limits, parking distances, permit rules and alcohol limits: Alabama, Alaska, Arizona, Arkansas, California, Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Florida, Georgia, Hawaii, Idaho, Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Kansas, Kentucky, Louisiana, Maine, Maryland, Massachusetts, Michigan, Minnesota, Mississippi, Missouri, Montana, Nebraska, Nevada, New Hampshire, New Jersey, New Mexico, New York, North Carolina, North Dakota, Ohio, Oklahoma, Oregon, Pennsylvania, Rhode Island, South Carolina, South Dakota, Tennessee, Texas, Utah, Vermont, Virginia, Washington, West Virginia, Wisconsin, Wyoming. More are added as we work through the official manuals.

Are these the exact questions on my test?

No one outside the DMV has the exact test. These questions cover the same facts from the same official manual your state tests you on.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in the browser on any phone or computer and keeps working offline once opened. You can add it to your home screen like an app.

What about a commercial license (CDL)?

There is a CDL Permit Prep app for the General Knowledge, Air Brakes and Combination Vehicles tests. It is free for one hour, then $10 for 12 months.
