Practice questions with answers

1. A North Carolina Level One permit holder must log at least how many hours of driving, and how many at night?

  1. 40 hours, including at least 6 at night
  2. 60 hours, including at least 10 at night
  3. 30 hours, including at least 5 at night
  4. 50 hours, including at least 10 at night
Show answer

B. 60 hours, including at least 10 at night

The driving log must show a minimum of 60 hours, with at least 10 hours at night.

2. During the first six months of a North Carolina Level One permit, when may you drive with your supervising driver?

  1. Between 5 a.m. and 9 p.m.
  2. Only in daylight on weekends
  3. Between 6 a.m. and midnight
  4. At any time
Show answer

A. Between 5 a.m. and 9 p.m.

For the first six months the permit allows driving between 5 a.m. and 9 p.m. with the supervising driver. After six months you may drive at any time with a supervising driver.

3. How long must a North Carolina Level One permit be held before applying for a Limited Provisional License?

  1. Three months
  2. Six months
  3. Twelve months
  4. Nine months
Show answer

D. Nine months

You must hold the Level One permit for nine months before applying for the Limited Provisional License.

4. A North Carolina provisional driver who is driving unsupervised may generally carry how many passengers under 21 from outside the household?

  1. No limit
  2. One
  3. None
  4. Two
Show answer

B. One

When driving unsupervised there may be one passenger under 21 who is not a member of the driver's household, with a narrow exception for a student being driven to or from school.

5. In North Carolina you should not park within how many feet of the curb line of an intersecting street?

  1. 10 feet
  2. 50 feet
  3. 25 feet
  4. 15 feet
Show answer

C. 25 feet

Do not park within 25 feet of the curb line of an intersecting street, or within 15 feet of the right-of-way lines if there is no curb.

6. In North Carolina you should not park within how many feet of a fire hydrant or the entrance to a fire station?

  1. 20 feet
  2. 10 feet
  3. 30 feet
  4. 15 feet
Show answer

D. 15 feet

The handbook lists 15 feet from a fire hydrant or fire station entrance.

7. In North Carolina you may not park within how many feet of an emergency vehicle stopped to investigate a crash or give assistance?

  1. 50 feet
  2. 25 feet
  3. 500 feet
  4. 100 feet
Show answer

D. 100 feet

Do not park within 100 feet of an emergency vehicle that has stopped to investigate an accident or give assistance.

8. Outside city limits in North Carolina, you may not park within how many feet of a fire or fire truck?

  1. 1,000 feet
  2. 400 feet
  3. 200 feet
  4. 100 feet
Show answer

B. 400 feet

The rule is one block inside city limits and 400 feet outside the city.

9. Parking on a North Carolina highway shoulder is against the law unless approaching drivers from both directions can see the vehicle for at least

  1. 200 feet
  2. 50 feet
  3. 500 feet
  4. 100 feet
Show answer

A. 200 feet

A vehicle parked on the shoulder must be visible from both directions for at least 200 feet.

10. When a train is approaching a North Carolina railroad crossing, where must you stop?

  1. Anywhere before the crossing sign
  2. At the rail
  3. Within 50 feet, but not less than 15 feet, from the nearest rail
  4. At least 200 feet away
Show answer

C. Within 50 feet, but not less than 15 feet, from the nearest rail

Stop within 50 feet but not less than 15 feet from the nearest rail and do not proceed until it is safe.

11. In North Carolina you may not pass on a curve or hill where you cannot see at least how far ahead?

  1. 100 feet
  2. 1,000 feet
  3. 200 feet
  4. 500 feet
Show answer

D. 500 feet

Passing is prohibited on any curve or hill where the driver cannot see at least 500 feet ahead.

12. With high-beam headlights on at night, the North Carolina handbook says you should be able to see a person how far away?

  1. 1,000 feet
  2. 200 feet
  3. 500 feet
  4. 75 feet
Show answer

B. 200 feet

High beams should let you see a person 200 feet away. Low beams should show someone 75 feet away.

13. The North Carolina handbook says everyone's driving is impaired at what blood alcohol concentration?

  1. 0.05 percent
  2. 0.08 percent
  3. 0.10 percent
  4. 0.02 percent
Show answer

B. 0.08 percent

Everyone's driving is impaired at 0.08 percent, and many people are affected at much lower levels.

14. What may happen when a North Carolina driver accumulates seven points?

  1. Nothing until 12 points
  2. The driver may be assigned to a driver improvement clinic
  3. The license is revoked for a year
  4. The driver must retake the road test
Show answer

B. The driver may be assigned to a driver improvement clinic

At seven points you may be assigned to a driver improvement clinic. Completing it takes three points off your record.

15. A North Carolina license may be suspended for accumulating how many points within three years?

  1. 12 points
  2. 8 points
  3. 7 points
  4. 15 points
Show answer

A. 12 points

As many as 12 points within a three-year period may bring a suspension.

16. After a North Carolina license is reinstated, a second suspension can follow from how many points within three years?

  1. 6 points
  2. 4 points
  3. 12 points
  4. 8 points
Show answer

D. 8 points

Eight points within three years after reinstatement can result in a second suspension.

17. How long is the first North Carolina suspension under the point system?

  1. 60 days
  2. 6 months
  3. 30 days
  4. 12 months
Show answer

A. 60 days

A first point-system suspension is 60 days.

18. On a North Carolina highway with two or more lanes in your direction, what must you do for a stopped emergency vehicle with its lights on?

  1. Move into a lane that is not the one nearest the vehicle
  2. Stop in your lane
  3. Keep your lane and speed
  4. Pass on the shoulder
Show answer

A. Move into a lane that is not the one nearest the vehicle

The Move Over law requires moving out of the lane nearest the emergency vehicle and staying out of it until you are safely clear.

19. What following distance rule does the North Carolina handbook teach?

  1. The one-second rule
  2. One car length per 20 mph
  3. The two-second rule
  4. The five-second rule
Show answer

C. The two-second rule

Allow two seconds between the time the vehicle ahead passes a point and the time you reach it.

20. On slippery pavement, the North Carolina handbook says to allow at least how much following distance?

  1. The normal distance
  2. Twice the normal distance
  3. One car length
  4. Half the normal distance
Show answer

B. Twice the normal distance

Stopping distance on slippery pavement is two to ten times longer, so allow at least twice the normal following distance.

21. In North Carolina, a child under what age and weight must be in a weight-appropriate child passenger restraint system?

  1. Under age 5 and under 40 pounds
  2. Under age 10 and under 100 pounds
  3. Under age 6 and under 60 pounds
  4. Under age 8 and under 80 pounds
Show answer

D. Under age 8 and under 80 pounds

A child under 8 and weighing less than 80 pounds must be secured in a weight-appropriate child restraint.

22. A new North Carolina resident has how many days to get a North Carolina license or learner permit?

  1. 30 days
  2. 90 days
  3. 10 days
  4. 60 days
Show answer

D. 60 days

New residents have 60 days after establishing residence.

23. Which of these is illegal for every driver in North Carolina?

  1. Talking with a passenger
  2. Using cruise control
  3. Listening to the radio
  4. Manually entering text or reading a text message or email while driving
Show answer

D. Manually entering text or reading a text message or email while driving

No driver may operate a vehicle while manually entering text to communicate or reading an email or text message.

24. In North Carolina, your license will be revoked for at least 30 days if you are convicted of driving

  1. 10 mph over the limit on any road
  2. Exactly 55 mph on an interstate
  3. 5 mph over the limit in town
  4. More than 15 mph over the limit while going faster than 55 mph
Show answer

D. More than 15 mph over the limit while going faster than 55 mph

Driving more than 15 mph over the speed limit at a speed above 55 mph brings a revocation of at least 30 days.

25. How many driver license points does running a stop sign add in North Carolina?

  1. 1 point
  2. 3 points
  3. 2 points
  4. 5 points
Show answer

B. 3 points

Running a stop sign, running a red light and speeding over 55 mph are each 3-point violations.
