Practice questions with answers

1. What is the minimum age to apply for an Oregon instruction permit?

  1. 16
  2. 15 and a half
  3. 14
  4. 15
Show answer

D. 15

You must be at least 15 for a permit and 16 for a license.

2. If you are under 18, how long must you hold a permit before an Oregon driver license?

  1. At least 6 months
  2. At least 3 months
  3. At least 30 days
  4. At least 1 year
Show answer

A. At least 6 months

Under 18 you must have held a permit for at least 6 months.

3. How much supervised driving does Oregon require for a driver under 18 without a traffic safety course?

  1. 100 hours
  2. 40 hours
  3. 60 hours
  4. 50 hours
Show answer

A. 100 hours

You need 100 hours, or 50 hours plus an ODOT-approved traffic safety education course.

4. At what BAC does Oregon law presume you are impaired?

  1. 0.10% or more
  2. 0.08% or more
  3. 0.12% or more
  4. 0.05% or more
Show answer

B. 0.08% or more

At 0.08% the law presumes impairment, but you can be arrested for DUII below that.

5. What does Oregon require of drivers 18 or older who use a cell phone?

  1. Use only at red lights
  2. A hands-free accessory
  3. Speakerphone held in the hand
  4. Nothing
Show answer

B. A hands-free accessory

Drivers 18 or older must use a hands-free accessory that needs only minimal use of a finger.

6. In Oregon traffic, use your turn signal at least how far before a turn or lane change?

  1. 50 feet
  2. 100 feet
  3. 300 feet
  4. 200 feet
Show answer

B. 100 feet

Signal at least 100 feet before the turn or lane change.

7. In Oregon you may not park within how many feet of a fire hydrant?

  1. 15 feet
  2. 20 feet
  3. 10 feet
  4. 5 feet
Show answer

C. 10 feet

Parking is prohibited within 10 feet of a fire hydrant.

8. In Oregon you may not park within how many feet of a crosswalk at an intersection?

  1. 10 feet
  2. 30 feet
  3. 15 feet
  4. 20 feet
Show answer

D. 20 feet

The limit is 20 feet from a marked or unmarked crosswalk at an intersection.

9. In Oregon you may not park within how many feet of the nearest rail of a railroad crossing?

  1. 100 feet
  2. 15 feet
  3. 50 feet
  4. 30 feet
Show answer

C. 50 feet

The limit is 50 feet.

10. In Oregon, you must dim high beams when an oncoming vehicle is within

  1. 350 feet
  2. 1,000 feet
  3. 100 feet
  4. 500 feet
Show answer

D. 500 feet

Dim within 500 feet of an oncoming vehicle and within 350 feet when following.

11. In Oregon, dim your lights when following another vehicle within

  1. 500 feet
  2. 100 feet
  3. 350 feet
  4. 200 feet
Show answer

C. 350 feet

The following distance for dimming is 350 feet.

12. When must headlights be on in Oregon?

  1. Only in rain
  2. Only on highways
  3. Only after 10 p.m.
  4. From sunset to sunrise, and whenever you cannot see people or vehicles 1,000 feet ahead
Show answer

D. From sunset to sunrise, and whenever you cannot see people or vehicles 1,000 feet ahead

Lights are required sunset to sunrise and in conditions that limit sight to 1,000 feet.

13. What does the Oregon manual define as a safe following distance?

  1. 2 to 4 seconds
  2. 10 seconds
  3. Half a second
  4. 1 second
Show answer

A. 2 to 4 seconds

A safe distance is 2-4 seconds, and 4 seconds or more above 30 mph.

14. What is the Oregon speed limit in alleys and narrow residential areas?

  1. 15 miles per hour
  2. 20 miles per hour
  3. 25 miles per hour
  4. 10 miles per hour
Show answer

A. 15 miles per hour

The basic speed designations set 15 mph in alleys and narrow residential areas.

15. What is the Oregon speed limit in a business district or school speed zone?

  1. 20 miles per hour
  2. 30 miles per hour
  3. 25 miles per hour
  4. 15 miles per hour
Show answer

A. 20 miles per hour

Business districts and school speed zones are 20 mph.

16. In Oregon, is it legal to turn left onto a two-way road against a red light?

  1. No
  2. Yes, at night
  3. Yes
  4. Yes, after stopping
Show answer

A. No

The manual states it is illegal to make a left turn onto a two-way road against a red light.
