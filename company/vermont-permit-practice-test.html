Practice questions with answers

1. How old must you be to take the Vermont Learner's Permit test?

  1. 16
  2. 14
  3. 15
  4. 15 and a half
Show answer

C. 15

You must be at least 15 years old to take the Learner's Permit test.

2. How long must you hold a Vermont Learner's Permit before a Junior Driver's License?

  1. 2 years
  2. At least one year
  3. 6 months
  4. 3 months
Show answer

B. At least one year

An individual must possess a Learner's Permit for at least one year before obtaining a Junior Driver's License.

3. How much practice driving must you complete before a Vermont Junior Driver's License?

  1. 50 hours, with at least 10 at night
  2. 60 hours, with at least 15 at night
  3. 20 hours, any time
  4. 40 hours, with at least 10 at night
Show answer

D. 40 hours, with at least 10 at night

In addition to driver education, you must complete 40 hours of practice, with at least 10 hours of nighttime driving.

4. How does Vermont define nighttime driving for practice hours?

  1. Midnight to 5 AM
  2. From 30 minutes after sunset to 30 minutes before sunrise
  3. Any time after 6 PM
  4. After the streetlights come on
Show answer

B. From 30 minutes after sunset to 30 minutes before sunrise

Nighttime driving is from 30 minutes after sunset to 30 minutes before sunrise.

5. During the first three months of a Vermont Junior Driver's License, who may ride with the driver?

  1. No one, unless a licensed parent, guardian or qualified adult supervisor is in the car
  2. Anyone with a seat belt
  3. Any family member
  4. Up to two friends
Show answer

A. No one, unless a licensed parent, guardian or qualified adult supervisor is in the car

For the first three months you must drive alone unless a licensed and unimpaired parent, guardian or qualified driver is aboard.

6. After how long with a Vermont Junior Driver's License is there no limit on the number of passengers (beyond seat belts)?

  1. Until age 18
  2. Three months
  3. One year
  4. Six months
Show answer

D. Six months

After holding the Junior License for six months, there is no limit on passengers, but never more passengers than safety belts.

7. For how long may a Vermont Junior License holder not drive as part of a job?

  1. One year after the Junior License is issued
  2. There is no such rule
  3. Until age 21
  4. Three months
Show answer

A. One year after the Junior License is issued

A Junior License holder may not drive in the course of employment for one year after issuance, for example as a delivery driver.

8. What is the Vermont limit on drivers under 21 for alcohol?

  1. 0.02 or more
  2. 0.05 or more
  3. 0.08 or more
  4. 0.10 or more
Show answer

A. 0.02 or more

Drivers under 21 with an alcohol concentration of .02 or more can face penalties; .08 is impaired for all drivers.

9. What is the maximum speed on Vermont roads unless posted otherwise?

  1. 55 mph
  2. 50 mph
  3. 45 mph
  4. 60 mph
Show answer

B. 50 mph

The maximum speed on all Vermont roads is 50 mph, or as posted.

10. What is the maximum speed on a Vermont interstate unless posted otherwise?

  1. 65 mph
  2. 70 mph
  3. 75 mph
  4. 60 mph
Show answer

A. 65 mph

The maximum speed for any vehicle on an interstate in Vermont is 65 mph, or as posted.

11. How far before a turn or lane change must you signal in Vermont?

  1. At least 100 feet
  2. At least 500 feet
  3. At least 200 feet
  4. At least 50 feet
Show answer

A. At least 100 feet

The law requires a turn signal not less than 100 feet before a turn or lane change, about three to five seconds.

12. How close may you park to a fire hydrant in Vermont?

  1. No closer than 6 feet
  2. No closer than 20 feet
  3. No closer than 10 feet
  4. No closer than 15 feet
Show answer

A. No closer than 6 feet

Do not park within 6 feet of a fire hydrant.

13. How close may you park to the nearest rail of a railroad crossing in Vermont?

  1. No closer than 100 feet
  2. No closer than 20 feet
  3. No closer than 50 feet
  4. No closer than 30 feet
Show answer

C. No closer than 50 feet

Do not park on railroad tracks or within 50 feet of the nearest rail of a crossing.

14. What must you do when an emergency vehicle with flashing blue or red lights or a siren approaches in Vermont?

  1. Stop in your lane
  2. Speed up to get out of the way
  3. Turn on your hazard lights and continue
  4. Pull to the right side of the road and stop until it passes
Show answer

D. Pull to the right side of the road and stop until it passes

You must pull over to the right side of the road and stop, and stay stopped until the vehicle has gone by.

15. How much space must you leave when passing a bicyclist in Vermont?

  1. At least four feet
  2. At least six feet
  3. At least three feet
  4. At least two feet
Show answer

A. At least four feet

Leave at least four feet of passing space between the right side of your vehicle and a bicyclist.

16. What child seat does Vermont require for a child under two?

  1. A seat belt only
  2. A booster seat
  3. A forward-facing seat
  4. A rear-facing car seat with a harness
Show answer

D. A rear-facing car seat with a harness

A child under two must be secured in a federally approved rear-facing car seat with a harness.

17. Within how many days must you report a name or address change to the Vermont DMV?

  1. 90 days
  2. 10 days
  3. 60 days
  4. 30 days
Show answer

D. 30 days

You must report a name or address change to the DMV within 30 days.
