Practice questions with answers

1. Unless a sign says otherwise, the speed limit in a Colorado residence district is

  1. 25 mph
  2. 20 mph
  3. 35 mph
  4. 30 mph
Show answer

D. 30 mph

The handbook lists 30 mph in any residence district and 25 mph in any business district.

2. Unless a sign says otherwise, the speed limit in a Colorado business district is

  1. 30 mph
  2. 35 mph
  3. 25 mph
  4. 15 mph
Show answer

C. 25 mph

Business districts are 25 mph; residence districts are 30 mph.

3. Unless posted otherwise, the speed limit on narrow, winding mountain highways and blind curves is

  1. 15 mph
  2. 30 mph
  3. 40 mph
  4. 20 mph
Show answer

D. 20 mph

Open mountain highways are 40 mph; narrow, winding ones and blind curves are 20 mph.

4. Unless posted otherwise, the speed limit on open mountain highways in Colorado is

  1. 40 mph
  2. 20 mph
  3. 65 mph
  4. 55 mph
Show answer

A. 40 mph

The handbook lists 40 mph on open mountain highways.

5. Two vehicles meet on a steep, narrow mountain road too tight for both. Who must yield?

  1. The vehicle on the left
  2. The vehicle going downhill
  3. The vehicle going uphill
  4. The larger vehicle
Show answer

B. The vehicle going downhill

The downhill driver backs up to a wider place or stops, unless it is more practical for the uphill driver to pull into a turnout.

6. How far from a school bus with flashing red lights must you stop in Colorado?

  1. At least 20 feet
  2. One car length
  3. At least 5 feet
  4. At least 50 feet
Show answer

A. At least 20 feet

Stop at least 20 feet before the bus and stay stopped until the red lights stop flashing.

7. In Colorado, you do not have to stop for a school bus with red lights flashing when

  1. You are on the opposite side of an undivided road
  2. A median or other physical barrier separates you from the bus
  3. The bus is at an intersection
  4. You are in a hurry
Show answer

B. A median or other physical barrier separates you from the bus

Without a barrier, you must stop whether the bus is on your side, the other side, or at an intersection.

8. A vehicle is stopped on a Colorado highway with its hazard lights flashing. If you cannot move over a lane, you must slow to

  1. At least 20 mph below the posted speed limit
  2. 5 mph below the limit
  3. The posted limit
  4. 10 mph below the limit
Show answer

A. At least 20 mph below the posted speed limit

Colorado's Move Over Law covers any stationary vehicle with hazards flashing.

9. In Colorado, a driver may be charged with DWAI (driving while ability impaired) starting at a blood alcohol level of

  1. 0.10%
  2. 0.08%
  3. 0.02%
  4. 0.05%
Show answer

D. 0.05%

DWAI begins at 0.05%. DUI per se applies above 0.08%.

10. A Colorado driver under 21 can lose the license for driving with a blood alcohol level over

  1. 0.04%
  2. 0.08%
  3. 0.05%
  4. 0.02%
Show answer

D. 0.02%

The limit is .02 for drivers under 21 and .04 for commercial drivers.

11. Under Colorado's Express Consent Law, if you refuse a chemical test your license is

  1. Not affected
  2. Revoked for one year
  3. Suspended for 30 days
  4. Revoked for life
Show answer

B. Revoked for one year

You also must install an ignition interlock device afterward.

12. In Colorado, you must dim your high beams before coming within how far of an oncoming vehicle?

  1. 500 feet
  2. 200 feet
  3. 1,000 feet
  4. 100 feet
Show answer

A. 500 feet

Dim within 500 feet of an oncoming vehicle and within 200 feet when following one.

13. When following another vehicle in Colorado, you must use low beams within

  1. 50 feet
  2. 1,000 feet
  3. 500 feet
  4. 200 feet
Show answer

D. 200 feet

500 feet applies to oncoming vehicles; 200 feet applies when following.

14. Colorado law requires headlights from sunset to sunrise or when visibility is less than

  1. 200 feet
  2. 1,000 feet
  3. 500 feet
  4. One mile
Show answer

B. 1,000 feet

Parked vehicles beside the road need parking lights in the same conditions.

15. In Colorado, you may not park within how many feet of a fire hydrant?

  1. 15 feet
  2. 30 feet
  3. 5 feet
  4. 50 feet
Show answer

A. 15 feet

It is 15 feet from a hydrant and 30 feet from a traffic signal, stop sign or railroad crossing.

16. In Colorado, you may not park within how many feet of a stop sign or traffic signal?

  1. 30 feet
  2. 100 feet
  3. 15 feet
  4. 10 feet
Show answer

A. 30 feet

The same 30 feet applies to railroad crossings.

17. When parallel parked in Colorado, your vehicle must be within how far of the curb?

  1. 18 inches
  2. 24 inches
  3. 6 inches
  4. 12 inches
Show answer

D. 12 inches

Park within 12 inches of the curb or as close as possible to the edge of the shoulder.

18. In urban areas of Colorado, you must signal continuously for at least how far before a turn or lane change?

  1. 50 feet
  2. 300 feet
  3. 200 feet
  4. 100 feet
Show answer

D. 100 feet

It is 100 feet in urban areas and 200 feet on four-lane highways with limits above 40 mph.

19. Under Colorado law, an adult driver may use a mobile phone while driving only

  1. When traffic is light
  2. When stopped at a red light
  3. With a hands-free accessory or in an emergency
  4. On roads under 45 mph
Show answer

C. With a hands-free accessory or in an emergency

Texting and manual use of a mobile device while driving are prohibited.

20. For the first six months after a Colorado driver under 18 gets a license, the driver may carry

  1. One passenger under 21
  2. Only passengers under 16
  3. Any number of passengers
  4. No passengers under 21, unless a parent or licensed adult is in the vehicle
Show answer

D. No passengers under 21, unless a parent or licensed adult is in the vehicle

After six months, one passenger under 21 is allowed. Siblings and medical emergencies are exceptions.

21. During the first year of a Colorado license, a driver under 18 may not drive alone between

  1. 10 p.m. and 6 a.m.
  2. 11 p.m. and 4 a.m.
  3. 9 p.m. and 5 a.m.
  4. Midnight and 5 a.m.
Show answer

D. Midnight and 5 a.m.

Exceptions include driving to school or work with a signed statement, and medical emergencies.

22. A Colorado minor must hold an instruction permit for how long before applying for a license?

  1. 12 months
  2. 3 months
  3. 6 months
  4. 18 months
Show answer

A. 12 months

The minor must also log 50 hours of driving, 10 of them at night.

23. A Colorado minor's driving log must show how many hours of driving experience?

  1. 50 hours, 10 of them at night
  2. 30 hours, none at night
  3. 20 hours, 5 at night
  4. 100 hours, 20 at night
Show answer

A. 50 hours, 10 of them at night

The signed log is turned in when applying for the license.

24. In Colorado, a two-wheeled motorcycle may pass stopped vehicles in the same lane (lane filtering) at no more than

  1. 35 mph
  2. 25 mph
  3. 5 mph
  4. 15 mph
Show answer

D. 15 mph

Vehicles in the rider's lane and the lanes next to it must be stopped, and there must be room to pass safely.

25. In a Colorado roundabout, drivers must yield the right-of-way to

  1. Only vehicles on the right
  2. No one once inside
  3. Smaller cars
  4. Trucks, buses, emergency vehicles and RVs
Show answer

D. Trucks, buses, emergency vehicles and RVs

Large vehicles need extra room, so Colorado law gives them the right-of-way when entering, exiting or driving in a roundabout.

26. In Colorado, you must yield to a transit bus that is

  1. Carrying passengers
  2. In the left lane
  3. Signaling to enter a traffic lane with its rear yield sign lit
  4. Stopped at any bus stop
Show answer

C. Signaling to enter a traffic lane with its rear yield sign lit

Let the bus merge when its yield sign is illuminated.

27. In Colorado, it is illegal to pass a snow plow when

  1. Plows are working in a staggered diagonal formation
  2. You are on a two-lane road
  3. It is snowing
  4. The plow is on the shoulder
Show answer

A. Plows are working in a staggered diagonal formation

Blowing snow can hide the plows, and passing between them is dangerous.

28. In Colorado, you must tell the DMV of a change of name or address within

  1. 30 days
  2. 60 days
  3. 90 days
  4. 10 days
Show answer

A. 30 days

The handbook gives 30 days to notify the Division of Motor Vehicles.
