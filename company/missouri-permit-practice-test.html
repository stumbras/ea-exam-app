Practice questions with answers

1. How many questions are on the Missouri Class F written test, and how many must you answer correctly?

  1. 20 questions, 16 correct
  2. 25 questions, 20 correct
  3. 50 questions, 40 correct
  4. 30 questions, 24 correct
Show answer

B. 25 questions, 20 correct

The written test has 25 multiple-choice questions and you must answer 20 correctly.

2. At what age can you get a Missouri instruction permit?

  1. 15 and a half
  2. 14
  3. 15
  4. 16
Show answer

C. 15

The instruction permit is the first step of the Graduated Driver License program, at age 15.

3. How long must a Missouri teen hold an instruction permit before an intermediate license?

  1. At least 30 days
  2. At least one year
  3. At least six months (182 days)
  4. At least three months
Show answer

C. At least six months (182 days)

The permit must be held a minimum of six months, starting the day after it is issued.

4. How many hours of driving instruction must a Missouri teen complete for an intermediate license?

  1. 20 hours, including 5 at night
  2. 50 hours, including 10 at night
  3. 60 hours, including 10 at night
  4. 40 hours, including at least 10 at night
Show answer

D. 40 hours, including at least 10 at night

The requirement is 40 hours with a parent, guardian or certified trainer, including at least ten hours of nighttime driving.

5. During the first six months of a Missouri intermediate license, how many passengers under 19 who are not immediate family may you carry?

  1. No more than three
  2. None
  3. No more than one
  4. No limit
Show answer

C. No more than one

In the first six months, no more than one passenger under 19 who is not immediate family. After that, no more than three.

6. A Missouri intermediate license holder may not drive alone during which hours, except for school, work or an emergency?

  1. 1:00 a.m. to 5:00 a.m.
  2. 10 p.m. to 6 a.m.
  3. Midnight to 4 a.m.
  4. 11 p.m. to 5 a.m.
Show answer

A. 1:00 a.m. to 5:00 a.m.

Driving alone between 1:00 a.m. and 5:00 a.m. is not allowed unless accompanied by a licensed driver 21 or older.

7. In Missouri, your license is taken at a traffic stop if your BAC is

  1. .10% or more for everyone
  2. .08% or more, or .02% or more for minors
  3. .08% or more for everyone, including minors
  4. .05% or more for everyone
Show answer

B. .08% or more, or .02% or more for minors

At .08% or more, or .02% or more for minors, the officer takes the license and gives a notice of suspension or revocation.

8. Under Missouri's Abuse and Lose law, a driver younger than 21 can lose their license for how long for an alcohol-related traffic offense?

  1. One year
  2. 30 days
  3. 90 days
  4. 10 days
Show answer

C. 90 days

If you are younger than 21, your license may be taken away for 90 days.

9. What does the Missouri Driver Guide say about texting?

  1. It is legal for drivers over 21
  2. It is against the law for any person to read, write or send a text or electronic message while operating a vehicle
  3. It is legal at stop lights
  4. It is only illegal in school zones
Show answer

B. It is against the law for any person to read, write or send a text or electronic message while operating a vehicle

Texting takes visual, manual and mental attention and is against the law for any person operating a vehicle.

10. In Missouri, you should signal at least how far before you turn?

  1. 50 feet
  2. 300 feet
  3. 200 feet
  4. 100 feet
Show answer

D. 100 feet

Get into the proper lane and signal at least 100 feet before you turn.

11. In a Missouri center left-turn lane, a vehicle may not travel more than

  1. 1,000 feet
  2. 100 feet
  3. 200 feet
  4. 500 feet
Show answer

D. 500 feet

The lane is for left turns only. Do not travel in it for more than 500 feet.

12. Missouri school bus amber warning lights begin flashing how far before the bus comes to a designated stop?

  1. 100 feet
  2. 500 feet
  3. 200 feet
  4. 1,000 feet
Show answer

B. 500 feet

Amber lights flash 500 feet before the stop. When the bus stops, the red lights and stop arm are activated.

13. On a Missouri two-lane road, when a school bus has its red lights flashing and stop arm out, who must stop?

  1. Only oncoming traffic
  2. Only traffic behind the bus
  3. No one, if no children are visible
  4. Oncoming and following traffic
Show answer

D. Oncoming and following traffic

Oncoming and following traffic must stop before they reach the bus on a two-lane road.

14. When approaching a stationary emergency vehicle with lights displayed on a Missouri road with at least four lanes, you must

  1. Stop in your lane
  2. Change into a lane not next to the vehicle if it is safe
  3. Speed up and pass quickly
  4. Use the shoulder
Show answer

B. Change into a lane not next to the vehicle if it is safe

Proceed with caution and change lanes away from the vehicle. If you cannot, reduce speed and proceed with due caution.

15. In Missouri, you may not pass when approaching within how many feet of an intersection or railroad crossing?

  1. 500 feet
  2. 100 feet
  3. 50 feet
  4. 200 feet
Show answer

B. 100 feet

Passing is prohibited within 100 feet of an intersection or railroad grade crossing, or where the view is obstructed within 100 feet of a bridge, viaduct or tunnel.

16. When must headlights be on at night in Missouri?

  1. From sunset to midnight
  2. Only outside city limits
  3. Only after 9 p.m.
  4. From one-half hour after sunset until one-half hour before sunrise
Show answer

D. From one-half hour after sunset until one-half hour before sunrise

Turn headlights on from half an hour after sunset until half an hour before sunrise.

17. What following distance rule does the Missouri Driver Guide teach?

  1. The three second rule
  2. One car length per 20 mph
  3. The five second rule
  4. The one second rule
Show answer

A. The three second rule

Choose an object ahead and count. You should not reach it before three seconds have passed.

18. In Missouri, your driving privilege is suspended if you accumulate

  1. 6 points in 6 months
  2. 8 or more points in 18 months
  3. 4 points in 12 months
  4. 10 points in 36 months
Show answer

B. 8 or more points in 18 months

At 4 points in 12 months you get an advisory letter. At 8 or more in 18 months your privilege is suspended.

19. In Missouri, your driving privilege is revoked for one year if you accumulate

  1. 8 points in 18 months
  2. 10 points in 24 months
  3. 6 points in 12 months
  4. 12 or more points in 12 months
Show answer

D. 12 or more points in 12 months

Revocation for one year follows 12 points in 12 months, 18 in 24 months or 24 in 36 months.

20. In Missouri, children less than four years old must be secured in

  1. A booster seat only
  2. Any rear seat
  3. A child passenger restraint system appropriate for the child
  4. An adult seat belt
Show answer

C. A child passenger restraint system appropriate for the child

Children under four, regardless of weight, must be in an appropriate child passenger restraint system.
