Practice questions with answers

1. A round yellow sign with a black X and two R's means

  1. Road closed ahead
  2. Roundabout ahead
  3. Railroad crossing ahead
  4. Rest area ahead
Show answer

C. Railroad crossing ahead

It is the advance warning for a railroad crossing. Slow down, look and listen.

2. You feel sleepy while driving. The best thing to do is

  1. Drive faster to get home
  2. Pull off the road and rest
  3. Turn up the radio
  4. Open the window
Show answer

B. Pull off the road and rest

Nothing replaces sleep. Drowsy driving causes crashes.

3. A red circle with a red slash over a symbol means

  1. It applies to trucks only
  2. Use caution when doing it
  3. The action shown is required
  4. The action shown is not allowed
Show answer

D. The action shown is not allowed

For example, a slash over a left-turn arrow means no left turn.

4. A five-sided (pentagon) sign warns of a

  1. Railroad crossing
  2. Hospital zone
  3. School zone or school crossing
  4. Work zone
Show answer

C. School zone or school crossing

The pentagon shape is used for school warnings. Watch for children.

5. You have a green light and want to turn left. There is no arrow. You must

  1. Turn first because you have the green
  2. Stop and wait for an arrow
  3. Wait for a red light
  4. Yield to oncoming traffic and pedestrians
Show answer

D. Yield to oncoming traffic and pedestrians

A plain green light does not protect a left turn.

6. A flashing red light means

  1. Slow down and proceed
  2. Stop, then go when it is safe
  3. The signal is about to turn green
  4. Stop only for pedestrians
Show answer

B. Stop, then go when it is safe

Treat a flashing red light like a stop sign.

7. For most adult drivers, it is illegal to drive with a blood alcohol concentration of

  1. 0.08% or higher (0.05% in Utah)
  2. 0.15% or higher
  3. Any amount is legal if you feel fine
  4. 0.10% or higher
Show answer

A. 0.08% or higher (0.05% in Utah)

Drivers under 21 face much lower limits in every state.

8. Large trucks have big blind spots called

  1. No-zones
  2. Merge zones
  3. Draft zones
  4. Safe zones
Show answer

A. No-zones

If you cannot see the truck's mirrors, the driver cannot see you.

9. A pedestrian is crossing in a crosswalk in front of you. You must

  1. Drive around the pedestrian
  2. Stop or yield until the pedestrian is out of your path
  3. Honk and continue slowly
  4. Go first if you are in a hurry
Show answer

B. Stop or yield until the pedestrian is out of your path

Drivers must yield to pedestrians in crosswalks.

10. Your car starts to hydroplane on a wet road. You should

  1. Brake hard
  2. Turn the wheel sharply
  3. Speed up
  4. Ease off the gas and steer straight
Show answer

D. Ease off the gas and steer straight

Let the car slow until the tires grip again.

11. When passing a bicyclist, you should

  1. Honk and pass quickly
  2. Leave plenty of room, at least 3 feet in many states
  3. Stay behind for a mile
  4. Pass as close as you can
Show answer

B. Leave plenty of room, at least 3 feet in many states

Bicyclists have the same rights as other drivers.

12. Orange signs warn drivers about

  1. School crossings
  2. Construction and work zones
  3. Recreation areas
  4. Hospitals and services
Show answer

B. Construction and work zones

Orange means road work. Slow down and watch for workers.

13. At a stop sign with no stop line or crosswalk, stop

  1. Only if other cars are present
  2. In the middle of the intersection
  3. Before entering the intersection, where you can see cross traffic
  4. Ten car lengths back
Show answer

C. Before entering the intersection, where you can see cross traffic

Stop at the stop line first, then the crosswalk, then the edge of the intersection.

14. A red and white triangle pointing down means

  1. Yield the right-of-way
  2. Stop completely
  3. No passing zone
  4. School zone ahead
Show answer

A. Yield the right-of-way

Slow down, and stop if needed, to let other traffic and pedestrians go first.

15. Green highway signs give

  1. Work zone information
  2. Warnings of hazards
  3. Rules you must obey
  4. Directions and distances
Show answer

D. Directions and distances

Green is for guide signs. Blue shows services and brown shows parks and recreation.

16. A steady yellow light means

  1. Yield, then proceed
  2. Speed up to beat the red light
  3. Stop immediately in every case
  4. The light is about to turn red; stop if you can do so safely
Show answer

D. The light is about to turn red; stop if you can do so safely

If you are too close to stop safely, continue through with care.

17. Two solid yellow lines in the center of the road mean

  1. No passing from either direction
  2. A bike lane
  3. A one-way road
  4. Passing is allowed for both directions
Show answer

A. No passing from either direction

You may cross them only to turn left into a driveway or road where allowed.

18. When parking uphill next to a curb, turn your front wheels

  1. It does not matter
  2. Straight ahead
  3. Toward the curb
  4. Away from the curb
Show answer

D. Away from the curb

If the car rolls, the curb will stop it. Downhill, turn the wheels toward the curb.

19. What lowers the alcohol level in your body?

  1. Time
  2. A cold shower
  3. Fresh air
  4. Coffee
Show answer

A. Time

Only time lets your body remove alcohol.

20. A red, eight-sided sign means

  1. Stop completely
  2. Slow down and proceed
  3. Do not enter
  4. Yield to other traffic
Show answer

A. Stop completely

The octagon shape is used only for stop signs. Come to a full stop.

21. A yellow, diamond-shaped sign is a

  1. Guide sign
  2. Warning sign
  3. Service sign
  4. Regulatory sign
Show answer

B. Warning sign

Diamond signs warn of hazards or changes in the road ahead.

22. You miss your freeway exit. You should

  1. Stop and wait for a gap to cross
  2. Make a U-turn across the median
  3. Back up on the shoulder
  4. Go on to the next exit
Show answer

D. Go on to the next exit

Never back up or stop on a freeway.

23. A white rectangular sign with black letters usually

  1. Gives directions to services
  2. Marks a work zone
  3. Warns of a hazard ahead
  4. States a rule you must obey, such as a speed limit
Show answer

D. States a rule you must obey, such as a speed limit

White rectangles are regulatory signs.

24. When entering a roundabout, you must

  1. Yield to traffic entering behind you
  2. Stop even if it is empty
  3. Yield to traffic already in the circle
  4. Enter at full speed
Show answer

C. Yield to traffic already in the circle

Traffic moves counterclockwise. Enter when there is a gap.

25. A flashing yellow light means

  1. Stop and wait for green
  2. Speed up to clear the intersection
  3. The light is broken, so ignore it
  4. Slow down and proceed with caution
Show answer

D. Slow down and proceed with caution

You do not have to stop, but be ready to.
