Practice questions with answers

1. If you are under 18, how long must you hold a Nevada instruction permit before applying for a license?

  1. At least 1 year
  2. At least 3 months
  3. At least 30 days
  4. At least 6 months
Show answer

D. At least 6 months

Under 18 you must hold the permit for at least 6 months.

2. If no driver's education class is available to a Nevada teen, how much behind-the-wheel experience is required?

  1. 40 hours
  2. 50 hours, 10 in the dark
  3. 60 hours
  4. 100 hours, 10 of them in the dark
Show answer

D. 100 hours, 10 of them in the dark

Without a class you need 100 hours, 10 of which must be in the dark.

3. If you are under 21 in Nevada, your license is affected when a chemical test shows an alcohol concentration of

  1. .02% but less than .08%
  2. .08% or more only
  3. .00% only
  4. .05% or more only
Show answer

A. .02% but less than .08%

Under 21, a result of .02% but less than .08% brings action against your driving privilege.

4. On Nevada city streets, signal your turn at least how far ahead?

  1. 300 feet
  2. 100 feet
  3. 50 feet
  4. 200 feet
Show answer

B. 100 feet

Signal at least 100 feet ahead on city streets and 300 feet on open highways.

5. On Nevada open highways, signal your turn at least how far ahead?

  1. 300 feet
  2. 500 feet
  3. 200 feet
  4. 100 feet
Show answer

A. 300 feet

The open-highway distance is 300 feet, about 30 car lengths.

6. In Nevada you may not park within how many feet of a fire hydrant where parallel parking is permitted?

  1. 25 feet
  2. 10 feet
  3. 15 feet
  4. 5 feet
Show answer

C. 15 feet

The limit is 15 feet with parallel parking and 20 feet where angle parking is permitted.

7. In Nevada you may not park within how many feet of a crosswalk?

  1. 10 feet
  2. 30 feet
  3. 20 feet
  4. 15 feet
Show answer

C. 20 feet

Do not park on a crosswalk or within 20 feet of one.

8. In Nevada you may not park within how many feet of a traffic control signal at the side of a highway?

  1. 10 feet
  2. 30 feet
  3. 50 feet
  4. 20 feet
Show answer

B. 30 feet

The limit is 30 feet.

9. In Nevada you may never park within how many feet of the nearest rail of a railroad?

  1. 15 feet
  2. 30 feet
  3. 50 feet
  4. 100 feet
Show answer

C. 50 feet

Never park within 50 feet of the nearest rail.

10. In Nevada, change to low beams at least how far before an oncoming vehicle?

  1. 300 feet
  2. 100 feet
  3. 1,000 feet
  4. 500 feet
Show answer

D. 500 feet

Dim at least 500 feet before oncoming vehicles and 300 feet before a vehicle you are following.

11. In Nevada, change to low beams at least how far before a vehicle you are following?

  1. 300 feet
  2. 500 feet
  3. 100 feet
  4. 200 feet
Show answer

A. 300 feet

The following distance is 300 feet.

12. When passing a bicycle in Nevada where no adjacent lane exists, the safe distance must be at least

  1. Two feet
  2. One foot
  3. Three feet
  4. Six feet
Show answer

C. Three feet

Pass to the left at a safe distance of at least three feet.

13. What are the Nevada school zone speed limits?

  1. 30 mph
  2. Either 15 or 25 mph
  3. 10 mph
  4. 20 mph only
Show answer

B. Either 15 or 25 mph

School zones are 15 or 25 mph, in effect on school days from a half hour before school begins to a half hour after it ends unless posted otherwise.

14. In Nevada, a left turn at a red light is allowed only when

  1. Turning from any street
  2. Turning from a one-way street onto another one-way street with traffic moving to the left
  3. A sign is missing
  4. No cars are coming
Show answer

B. Turning from a one-way street onto another one-way street with traffic moving to the left

Left on red is one-way to one-way only.

15. New Nevada residents must register their vehicles within how long of establishing residency?

  1. 90 days
  2. 30 days
  3. 10 days
  4. 60 days
Show answer

B. 30 days

Vehicles must be registered within 30 days.

16. What is the minimum age for a Nevada instruction permit?

  1. 15
  2. 15 and a half
  3. 14
  4. 16
Show answer

B. 15 and a half

To obtain an instruction permit you must be at least 15½ years old.

17. Who must accompany a Nevada Class C instruction permit holder?

  1. Any adult passenger
  2. A parent only
  3. A licensed driver 21 or older with at least one year of licensed driving experience
  4. Any licensed driver 18 or older
Show answer

C. A licensed driver 21 or older with at least one year of licensed driving experience

The accompanying driver must be 21 or older and have at least one year of licensed driving experience.

18. For the first 6 months, a Nevada driver licensed at 16 or 17 may not carry passengers

  1. Under 18, except immediate family
  2. Under 16 only
  3. Under 21, including family
  4. Of any age
Show answer

A. Under 18, except immediate family

Under NRS 483.2523, new drivers licensed at 16 or 17 may not carry passengers under 18, except immediate family, for the first 6 months.

19. Until age 18, a Nevada minor may not drive between

  1. 10 PM and 5 AM, unless driving to or from a scheduled event
  2. 9 PM and 6 AM
  3. Midnight and 6 AM
  4. 11 PM and 5 AM
Show answer

A. 10 PM and 5 AM, unless driving to or from a scheduled event

The curfew is 10 PM to 5 AM unless driving to or from a scheduled event, and lasts until age 18.

20. How much supervised driving must a Nevada teen complete with driver's education?

  1. 100 hours, 10 in the dark
  2. 30 hours
  3. 50 hours, 10 of them at night
  4. 40 hours, 10 at night
Show answer

C. 50 hours, 10 of them at night

Teens who complete an approved driver's education course need 50 hours of supervised experience, 10 at night.

21. In Nevada, may you hold a phone to talk while driving?

  1. No; voice calls need a hands-free device
  2. Yes, on rural roads
  3. Yes, at under 25 mph
  4. Yes, if you are 18 or older
Show answer

A. No; voice calls need a hands-free device

Using a handheld device for voice communication is prohibited unless used hands-free; texting is prohibited.

22. How many demerit points in 12 months suspend a Nevada license?

  1. 8
  2. 6
  3. 12
  4. 15
Show answer

C. 12

If you receive 12 or more points in any 12-month period, your license will be suspended.

23. How many points can Nevada traffic safety school remove?

  1. All points
  2. Up to three, once in 12 months
  3. None
  4. Up to six
Show answer

B. Up to three, once in 12 months

With 3 to 11 points, a DMV-approved traffic safety course removes up to three points; you may attend once in a 12-month period.

24. On a Nevada divided highway, when must you stop for a school bus with a flashing red signal?

  1. Never on a divided highway
  2. Only if children are on your side
  3. Always, from both directions
  4. Only when traveling in the same direction as the bus
Show answer

D. Only when traveling in the same direction as the bus

On divided highways you are only required to stop when traveling in the same direction as the school bus.

25. In Nevada, which children must ride in an approved child restraint?

  1. Children under 6 and under 57 inches tall
  2. Children under 12
  3. Children under 2
  4. Children under 40 pounds
Show answer

A. Children under 6 and under 57 inches tall

Children under age six and less than 57 inches tall must be in an approved child restraint system (NRS 484B.157).
