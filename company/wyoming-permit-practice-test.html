Practice questions with answers

1. At what age can you get a Wyoming instruction permit?

  1. 15
  2. 15 and a half
  3. 16
  4. 14
Show answer

A. 15

An instruction permit may be obtained at 15.

2. At what age can a Wyoming driver get an intermediate license?

  1. 16
  2. 16 and a half
  3. 17
  4. 15
Show answer

A. 16

A license may be issued at age 16 when the applicant acquires an intermediate license.

3. How many passengers under 18 who are not family may a Wyoming intermediate license holder carry without a licensed adult?

  1. Two
  2. One
  3. Three
  4. None
Show answer

B. One

An intermediate license holder may not carry more than one passenger under 18 who is not immediate family, unless also with a licensed driver 18 or older.

4. During which hours may a Wyoming intermediate license holder not drive, without an approved exception?

  1. 1 AM to 5 AM
  2. 10 PM to 5 AM
  3. Midnight to 6 AM
  4. 11 PM to 5 AM
Show answer

D. 11 PM to 5 AM

Intermediate license holders may not drive between 11 PM and 5 AM unless they meet an exception and carry the completed exception form.

5. What must every occupant do when a Wyoming intermediate license holder is driving?

  1. Be 21 or older
  2. Wear a seat belt
  3. Hold a driver's license
  4. Sit in the back seat
Show answer

B. Wear a seat belt

The intermediate license holder must ensure that all occupants of the vehicle are wearing seat belts.

6. What is the Wyoming speed limit in a school zone unless posted otherwise?

  1. 30 mph
  2. 25 mph
  3. 20 mph
  4. 15 mph
Show answer

C. 20 mph

Unless posted: school zones 20 mph, residential and business areas 30 mph.

7. What is the Wyoming speed limit in residential and business areas unless posted otherwise?

  1. 30 mph
  2. 35 mph
  3. 20 mph
  4. 25 mph
Show answer

A. 30 mph

Residential and business areas are 30 mph unless otherwise posted.

8. What is the Wyoming speed limit on secondary highways unless posted otherwise?

  1. 75 mph
  2. 70 mph
  3. 65 mph
  4. 60 mph
Show answer

B. 70 mph

Secondary highways are 70 mph, and interstates 75 or 80 mph, unless otherwise posted.

9. On a Wyoming two-lane road, how must you pass a stopped emergency or maintenance vehicle with lights on?

  1. Stop completely
  2. Sound your horn
  3. Slow down by at least 20 mph
  4. Keep the same speed
Show answer

C. Slow down by at least 20 mph

Move over to the farthest lane on an interstate, or reduce your speed by at least 20 mph on a two-lane road.

10. When may you pass a Wyoming school bus with flashing red lights?

  1. When no children are visible
  2. Only if a physical barrier or separate roadway lies between you and the bus
  3. When you are on a four-lane road
  4. Never
Show answer

B. Only if a physical barrier or separate roadway lies between you and the bus

You must stop from either direction; the only exception is a physical barrier or separate roadways between your vehicle and the bus.

11. How far before an intersection must you signal a turn in Wyoming?

  1. At least 50 feet
  2. At least 500 feet
  3. At least 200 feet
  4. At least 100 feet
Show answer

D. At least 100 feet

All drivers must signal when turning or changing lanes and at least 100 feet from an intersection.

12. How close may you park to a fire hydrant in Wyoming?

  1. No closer than 10 feet
  2. No closer than 5 feet
  3. No closer than 20 feet
  4. No closer than 15 feet
Show answer

D. No closer than 15 feet

Do not park within 15 feet of a fire hydrant.

13. When must headlights be on in Wyoming?

  1. Only on interstates
  2. Only after midnight
  3. Only in snow
  4. From half an hour after sunset to half an hour before sunrise, and whenever visibility is poor
Show answer

D. From half an hour after sunset to half an hour before sunrise, and whenever visibility is poor

Headlights must be used from one-half hour after sunset until one-half hour before sunrise, and at other times when light or weather is poor.

14. How soon should you dim your high beams before meeting an oncoming vehicle in Wyoming?

  1. At least 200 feet
  2. At least 100 feet
  3. At least 500 feet
  4. At least 1,000 feet
Show answer

C. At least 500 feet

You should dim at least 500 feet, about four to five seconds, before meeting an oncoming vehicle.

15. How much space should you leave when passing a bicyclist in Wyoming?

  1. No set distance
  2. At least 3 feet
  3. At least 1 foot
  4. At least 6 feet
Show answer

B. At least 3 feet

When space allows, maintain at least 3 feet of separation between your vehicle and the bicyclist.

16. What happens under Wyoming's administrative per se rule if your test shows 0.08% or more?

  1. A 90-day suspension, even if the DWUI charge is dismissed
  2. A suspension only if convicted
  3. A fine with no suspension
  4. Only a warning
Show answer

A. A 90-day suspension, even if the DWUI charge is dismissed

An alcohol concentration of 0.08% or greater brings a 90-day suspension; dismissal of the DWUI in court does not affect it.

17. Within how many days must you notify Wyoming Driver Services of an address change?

  1. 60 days
  2. 20 days
  3. 10 days
  4. 30 days
Show answer

C. 10 days

Licensees must notify the department in writing within 10 days of an address change.
