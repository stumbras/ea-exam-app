Privacy policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

This policy explains what Solving Life LLC ("we") collects, why, and what we do with it. It covers this website, our tax and bookkeeping services, and our apps.

Tax and bookkeeping clients

To prepare returns and keep books we collect the information you give us, such as names, identification numbers, income records and bank details for refunds or payments. We use it only to provide the services you asked for and to meet legal requirements. We do not sell it. We do not use or disclose tax return information for any other purpose without your signed written consent, as federal law requires. Some work may be done by contractors bound by the same rules; where work is done outside the United States, we ask for your written consent first.

Our apps

Our study apps and games keep your progress on your own device. They do not show ads. Without signing in they do not ask for your name. Signing in with Google is optional and is explained below.

Signing in with Google

Signing in is optional. If you sign in with Google in our apps, we receive your Google account ID, name and email address and use them only to save your study progress and load it on your other devices. Your saved progress is stored with our hosting provider, Cloudflare. We do not use it for ads and do not sell or share it. You can sign out at any time in the app, and you can ask us to delete your saved progress by writing to info@solvinglifellc.com.

Payments

Payments are handled by our payment providers and the app stores. We do not see or keep your full card number.

Website

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

Keeping information safe

We use two-factor sign-in on our business accounts, keep client files in protected business storage, and limit access to the people who need them.

Children

Our kids learning app is for use with a parent. It collects no personal information from children.

Your choices

You may ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete what we are not required to keep. Write to info@solvinglifellc.com.
