Practice questions with answers

1. What is the minimum age for a Maine learner's permit?

  1. 15 and a half
  2. 15
  3. 14
  4. 16
Show answer

B. 15

You must be at least 15 years of age.

2. Who may supervise a Maine learner's permit holder?

  1. A licensed driver at least 20 who has held a license for 2 years
  2. Any adult passenger
  3. Any licensed driver 18 or older
  4. Only a parent
Show answer

A. A licensed driver at least 20 who has held a license for 2 years

The permit holder must be accompanied by a licensed driver at least 20 who has held a valid license for 2 years.

3. How long must you hold a Maine learner's permit before the road test?

  1. 30 days
  2. 3 months
  3. 1 year
  4. 6 months
Show answer

D. 6 months

You must have held a learner's permit for 6 months from the date of issue.

4. How much supervised driving must a Maine learner complete?

  1. 40 hours
  2. 70 hours, including 10 at night
  3. 100 hours
  4. 50 hours, including 10 at night
Show answer

B. 70 hours, including 10 at night

A minimum of 70 hours of driving, including 10 hours of night driving.

5. During which hours may a new Maine driver under 18 not drive?

  1. 10 PM to 6 AM
  2. 1 AM to 6 AM
  3. 11 PM to 5 AM
  4. Midnight to 5 AM
Show answer

D. Midnight to 5 AM

Under 18, you may not drive between 12 AM and 5 AM during the restricted period.

6. How long do Maine's restrictions on new drivers under 18 last?

  1. 90 days
  2. Until age 18
  3. 1 year
  4. 270 days from passing the road test
Show answer

D. 270 days from passing the road test

These restrictions are in effect for 270 days from when the road test was passed.

7. How much alcohol may a Maine driver under 21 have before driving?

  1. Up to 0.08%
  2. None at all
  3. Up to 0.05%
  4. Up to 0.02%
Show answer

B. None at all

You may not drive after consuming any amount of alcohol if you are under 21.

8. Where must children age 12 and under ride in Maine?

  1. In the back seat
  2. Anywhere with a belt
  3. In a booster seat only
  4. In the front seat with an airbag
Show answer

A. In the back seat

All children age 12 and under must be in the back seat.

9. In Maine, children under what weight must ride in a child safety seat?

  1. 30 pounds
  2. 80 pounds
  3. 40 pounds
  4. 55 pounds
Show answer

D. 55 pounds

The operator must have a child under 55 pounds properly secured in a child safety seat.

10. What is the Maine speed limit in a business or residential district unless posted otherwise?

  1. 20 mph
  2. 25 mph
  3. 30 mph
  4. 35 mph
Show answer

B. 25 mph

25 mph in a business district, residential district or built-up area.

11. What is the Maine speed limit outside built-up areas unless posted otherwise?

  1. 50 mph
  2. 45 mph
  3. 55 mph
  4. 40 mph
Show answer

B. 45 mph

45 mph outside any business, residential or built-up area.

12. What is the Maine speed limit when passing a school during recess?

  1. 25 mph
  2. 15 mph
  3. 20 mph
  4. 10 mph
Show answer

B. 15 mph

15 mph when passing a school during recess.

13. When must you dim your high beams in Maine?

  1. Within 500 feet of an oncoming vehicle and within 300 feet when following
  2. Within 200 feet of any vehicle
  3. Only in fog
  4. Only in towns
Show answer

A. Within 500 feet of an oncoming vehicle and within 300 feet when following

Dim when an oncoming vehicle is within 500 feet or when following within 300 feet.

14. How close may you park to a fire hydrant in Maine?

  1. No closer than 5 feet
  2. No closer than 15 feet
  3. No closer than 10 feet
  4. No closer than 20 feet
Show answer

C. No closer than 10 feet

Do not park within 10 feet of a fire hydrant or within 15 feet of a crosswalk.

15. How far before a turn must you signal in Maine?

  1. At least 100 feet
  2. At least 50 feet
  3. At least 200 feet
  4. At least 300 feet
Show answer

A. At least 100 feet

Signal as early as you can, at least 100 feet before any change or turn.

16. How much space must you leave when passing a bicycle in Maine?

  1. No set distance
  2. At least one foot
  3. At least three feet
  4. At least five feet
Show answer

C. At least three feet

Maine law requires at least three feet of space when passing a bicycle.
